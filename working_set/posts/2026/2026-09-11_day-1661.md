Veblen had a term for activity whose uselessness is itself the point: conspicuous waste. This week brought the Peace Express, the name of a train that says everything about a government that thinks branding can substitute for substance. Passengers were handed ‘Kyiv regime’ T-shirts, an inside joke built around a Russian slur and worn by men who can’t tell a joke from a concession. By design or cosmic joke, the same week our anti-corruption bureau cracked a call-center racket operating inside the Prosecutor General’s own office — a snake pit inside the building meant to prosecute snake pits.
