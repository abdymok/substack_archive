*There is a lot I did not write about this week, and I have made my peace with this. The unwritten things accumulate quietly, like mold spreading on the damp, unheated walls of a Stalinist-era apartment, and they have their own dignity. The Vibe One projector, for instance, which we set up with the particular combination of triumph and humiliation that all technology eventually produces. The return, briefly, of electricity, which deserves its own small ceremony and received none. And no — not the Epstein files. Some silences are principled.*

*Oatmeal in a blanket evolved, as things do when left to their own devices, into borshch in a blanket. Twice, insulated by tin foil and a towel. There is a whole philosophy of civilization in that sentence, if you are willing to look for it, and I am, on day 1458, willing to look for almost anything.*

*Z appeared on screen this morning in yesterday evening’s pep talk looking, as my hookup might have said,* **very awake for the hour***. He did not mention the anniversary. Thirteen years. The kind of number that should stop a man mid-sentence, that should make the teleprompter itself pause out of respect. It did not. Instead, Z talked about a big drug bust. He will mention the anniversary today, maybe, in that smooth AI-curated fashion where the grief has been optimized and the pauses inserted by his fuckpig PR staff. A wreath-laying ceremony.*

*What Z was doing on February 18th, 19th, and 20th of 2014 remains, as they say, a mystery. History is full of such useful mysteries. They are almost always the most important part.*
