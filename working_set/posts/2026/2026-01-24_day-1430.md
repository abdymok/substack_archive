*International Law’s Greatest Hits* Jan 24, 2026

Systematic destruction of urban environments without genuine military necessity - urbicide - is not allowed under international law. The legal prohibition exists through the [Hague Convention](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/convention-protection-cultural-property-event-armed-conflict-regulations-execution-convention) protecting cultural property, the [Geneva Conventions](https://www.icrc.org/en/war-and-law/treaties-customary-law/geneva-conventions) prohibiting attacks on civilian objects, and the [Rome Statute](https://www.icc-cpi.int/sites/default/files/2024-05/Rome-Statute-eng.pdf) criminalizing such destruction as war crimes. The [Helsinki Final Act](https://www.osce.org/files/f/documents/5/c/39501.pdf) provides additional philosophical grounding for protecting urban heritage.

Yet the post-WWII international legal order - designed to prevent exactly these atrocities - has proven powerless when permanent Security Council members like Russia choose to violate it.

Which brings us to tonight.

Russia launched 396 aerial weapons at Ukraine - 2 Zircon anti-ship missiles, 12 Kh-22/Kh-32 cruise missiles, 6 Iskander-M/S-300 ballistic missiles, 1 Kh-59/69 guided aviation missile, and 375 attack drones. The main target was Kyiv, with strategic aviation deploying Kh-22/Kh-32 missiles against the capital.

Between 1:47 a.m. and 3:59 a.m., as peace talks were underway in Abu Dhabi, the attacks rolled through eight districts - Holosiivsky, Desnyansk, Solomiansky, Dniprovsky, Darnytsky. Two missiles and 18 drones struck 17 locations. Debris from intercepted drones hit 12 more. Office buildings struck, residential windows blown out, garages and a fuel truck set ablaze, a private medical clinic damaged.

I documented some of the strikes from my building, located in the middle of Kyiv’s mystic triangle, shuffling back and forth from under the covers of my bed down the dark hallway to the balcony. A view of the fireworks. I marveled at the precisionless destruction - the randomness of what gets hit, what doesn’t, who lives, who dies. I can’t publish the pictures. The images might be incorporated into Team Russia’s dataset to target future strikes. Even documentation becomes weaponizable intelligence.

One person killed, at least four injured and hospitalized in Kyiv. In Kharkiv, a maternity ward, a dormitory housing internally displaced persons, and a medical college were hit - 19 people wounded, including a child. Energy infrastructure destroyed again, leaving 1,200 apartment buildings without heat and entire neighborhoods without water in freezing temperatures.

The documents say this should never happen. But it does, and it keeps happening.

Z’s [Telegram response](https://t.me/V_Zelenskiy_official/12642) was characteristically data-driven: “Russia massively attacked our regions overnight - launching over 370 strike drones and 21 missiles of various types... In the capital and region, the main target for the Russians was energy infrastructure. Unfortunately, this attack took one person’s life. My condolences to relatives and loved ones.”

He thanked rescue workers, medics, utilities, and air defense forces, then pivoted: “We count on the reaction and assistance of all our partners. Every Patriot, NASAMS missile helps protect critical infrastructure and helps people get through the cold winter. We must definitely implement everything agreed upon with President Trump in Davos regarding air defense.”

This is Ukraine’s data-point commander-in-chief - the man who presided over back-office corruption in the very ministries tasked with protecting the population, who failed to prepare for Russia’s invasion despite explicit warnings, now reducing civilian casualties to talking points about partner assistance and Davos agreements. One person killed becomes a segue to weapons deliveries. Nineteen wounded in Kharkiv becomes leverage for Trump negotiations.

**Meanwhile, at Davos**

The word “urbicide” appeared nowhere. Neither did “war crimes.” Neither did “Geneva Conventions.”

Instead, Davos offered visions of AI “rehumanizing work,” land deals, and investment opportunities. [Palantir’s Louis Mosley](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VLLm9vFIdWo) waxed philosophical about how artificial intelligence would allow us to “refine meaning in what we do professionally.” Washington Post columnist [David Ignatius](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VLLm9vFIdWo) nodded approvingly.

[Mosley recounted](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VLLm9vFIdWo) how it all began: in May 2022, barely ten weeks after the invasion, Z invited Palantir’s CEO to Kyiv and made “a very compelling offer.” The “very charismatic” Z painted a picture of Ukraine becoming “the Israel of Europe” - surrounded by enemies, locked in “a long generational struggle,” dependent on military technology partnerships with industrial partners like Palantir.

In exchange for Palantir’s software, Z offered access to Ukraine’s “very very talented bench of engineering” - young men and women in uniform who could “build on our software and begin to wield it in ways that perhaps our other military customers around the world had not.”

Mosley gushed that they encountered engineers Palantir would be “thrilled to hire” working with “an ethic that can only come from an existential situation” where “building a new feature might make the difference between saving a friend’s life.” What began as a “philanthropic endeavor” quickly became a business relationship built on Ukraine’s desperation and “extraordinary data asset.”

Puhleaze.

Fast forward to Davos 2026, and [Z used his platform](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mQdmb7fxz-E) not to confront this grotesque circus with the reality of what his country faces, but to lecture Europe about its failures - as if the man who ignored explicit warnings, presided over strategic autophagé where officials looted during wartime, and botched defense preparations has earned the moral authority to scold others.

He closed by insisting “Groundhog Day must end,” apparently unaware that the real loop is his own: another forum, another speech, another performance substituting eloquence for strategy while his people freeze in warming centers.

Meanwhile, [Palantir CEO Alex Karp sat down with BlackRock CEO Larry Fink](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWCrw2wa3ls) for a cozy chat about how AI “will destroy humanities jobs” but create exciting opportunities for “vocational technicians” - people who “went to high school” and can now manage complex military systems. Karp praised his company’s Ukraine operations, claiming that “advanced algorithmic warfare systems” are equivalent to “tactical nuclear weapons.”

**The Data Asset**

As [Mosley celebrated](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VLLm9vFIdWo) Ukraine’s “extraordinary data asset” - “thousands, tens of thousands, even millions of hours of footage from drones” that is “completely unique in the world” - and Z delivered his latest theatrical critique of European resolve, the missiles kept falling on Kyiv.

The 1,200 apartment buildings without heat became a data point. The people who died became training sets. The wounded child in Kharkiv became a talking point for Patriot deliveries. The systematic destruction of a nation became a business opportunity and a stage for moral posturing.

**The Demographic Catastrophe**

Beyond the immediate violence lies something worse. In 2025, deaths in Ukraine exceeded births by 2.9 times - 485,300 deaths against only 168,780 births. In the worst-hit regions like Donetsk and Kherson oblasts, deaths outnumber births by over 11 to 1.

Ukraine’s population has collapsed from 48.5 million in 2001 to an estimated 35.8 million in mid-2024, with projections suggesting it could fall to 25.2 million by 2051.

This echoes the demographic hemorrhaging of early 20th century Ireland, when waves of emigration drained the country of its people. Like Ireland then, Ukraine faces not just immediate violence but the systematic destruction of its future - a people scattered across continents, their homeland deliberately made unlivable through the targeting of the very infrastructure that sustains life in winter.

**The Documents**

The international legal instruments designed to prevent exactly this - the Hague Convention, the Geneva Conventions, the Rome Statute, the Helsinki Final Act - gathered dust as reminders that law only matters when the powerful choose to enforce it.
