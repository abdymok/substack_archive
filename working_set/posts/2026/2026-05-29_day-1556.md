*Quotes (May 28, 2026)*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

In news of the weird, The New York Times [runs](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/28/world/europe/ukraine-war-street-style-fashion-military.html?searchResultPosition=9) a full spread about street style.

The Riot Division logo is “the silhouette of a hooded figure throwing a Molotov cocktail.[2](#footnote-2)” The piece calls this “an unofficial symbol of resistance.” It is also, if you are in a certain mood, a symbol of a brand that has correctly identified its market. The founder is serving in the military. This is mentioned in one subordinate clause, with the same weight as his founding date.

One does not ask the *Times* to be something it is not. It is a newspaper. It has sent a reporter, with a photographer, to document a real thing that is genuinely happening. The people quoted are real people saying things they presumably believe. None of this is false.

It is simply that the piece is about olive-colored skirts, and the war is about something else, and the distance between these two subjects is the only interesting thing — and the piece, having placed them side by side, declines to look.
