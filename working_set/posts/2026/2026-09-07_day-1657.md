The section of Volodymrska where the Security Service of Ukraine is located is blocked off this morning to allow construction crews to begin renovate the acting director’s office obliterated by a Russian kamikaze drone.

In other news, the official in charge of closing down fraud call centers for Ukraine’s Office of the Prosecutor has been remanded to pre-trial custody on UAH 120 million bail. Anti-corruption agencies on Friday [released recordings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UXNzknS8iHk) detailing what sounded like unscrupulous attempts to extort and hide oodles of cash from boiler rooms allowed to operate.

Prosecutor General Roman Kravchenko [denies involvement](https://www.pravda.com.ua/news/2026/09/06/8052186/) in the scheme.

> “The recordings clearly show that the anti-corruption bodies’ earlier claim about my involvement in receiving and laundering funds from illegal call centers has no confirmation whatsoever. Conversations are just conversations, and a photo of a safe with money is just a photo of a safe with money that has nothing to do with me or the Prosecutor General’s Office — according to lawyers, it’s simply a photo from a different criminal case,” Kravchenko [said](https://t.me/ruslan_kravchenko_ua/1209) on Telegram.

No one believes him.

Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner were in town yesterday. I didn’t pay much attention to their visit. The duo left on the Peace Express train yesterday evening.
