A squadron of Russian drones flew over Vilkhivets last month. You wouldn’t know it to look at the place.

[Image]

The village sits between Myronivka and Bohuslav on the Cherkasy border, named after the вільха — the alder tree — and the mighty Ros’ River runs down the hill across a field of oilseed rape that goes on until it doesn’t. This is where baby dragons live. You can walk miles in most directions without reaching any destination.

Large rabbits nibble on the crops and attract muscular raptors — eagles among them. Foxes, snakes, salamanders. Wild boar, a small population of deer and moose. A road to nowhere.

[Image]
