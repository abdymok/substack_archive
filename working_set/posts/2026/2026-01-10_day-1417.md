So, now we have Valerie, “based in Moscow,” [writing from Berlin](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/01/09/world/europe/putin-russia-ukraine-oreshnik-europe.html), telling us about the latest IRBM adventure in Lviv.

Word on the street is that Drump and Z are penciled in for a tête-à-tête at Davos later this month—because nothing says "resolving existential conflict" like a Swiss mountain resort where champagne costs more than a Javelin missile. Two performers, one stage. Perhaps Val will materialize too, lurking in the background like a ventriloquist's dummy waiting for her cue.

Meanwhile, I’m preparing for my own dramatic production: dawn patrol at [Hryshko Botanical Garden](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Mo89gaDqC4Z61dramatically8) with the [Small Penis Running Club](https://www.strava.com/clubs/1317730) (yes, that’s what we’re calling ourselves—intimidation through radical honesty). We’re blazing trails through the [wolf-inhabited sections](https://www.google.com/maps/@50.4154135,30.5613775,3a,75y,249.35h,77.2t/data=!3m8!1e1!3m6!1sAF1QipO_033isse9cx7j4ym6vYqKeE-ytKOXuIxT9bRb!2e10!3e11!6shttps:%2F%2Flh5.googleusercontent.com%2Fp%2FAF1QipO_033isse9cx7j4ym6vYqKeE-ytKOXuIxT9bRb%3Dw900-h600-k-no-pi-10-ya150-ro-0-fo100!7i10240!8i5120?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MDEwOC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) of the park, because apparently jogging past *Canis lupus* at 7 AM in sub-zero temperatures is my idea of reasonable life choices.

Sunrise isn’t until 8, but we start early—partially for the challenge, mostly because hypothermia waits for no one. I’m field-testing my gear, including [PRECISION ski socks](https://us.icebreaker.com/en-us/mens-socks/merino-ski-light-otc-socks/105084.html), marketed with the bold promise that my toes will remain merely *painfully cold* rather than *medically alarming ice sculpture*. If I survive, I’ll consider it a successful morning. If the wolves get me, at least I’ll be consumed daydreaming about the spectacle of Drump and Z plan discussing Ukraine’s future over Swiss fondue.

Sigh.
