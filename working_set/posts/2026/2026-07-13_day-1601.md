It’s Day 19 of 40 — [assuming June 25th counts as day one](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-1590) — of Z’s influence campaign against Russia. He arrives in Paris today, on the eve of the midway mark, for a Coalition of the Willing meeting chasing the thing Ukraine actually needs: Patriot interceptors, and a seat at Europe’s slow-motion effort to build an anti-ballistic air defense system of its own.

[Image]

*Photo c/o abdymok (July 10, 2026)*

Somewhere in the same frame of city as all this, a woman on Friday evening walked down a central Kyiv street with a champagne bottle already popped, cork gone, foam still finding its way down the neck. Nobody told her the government would fall over the weekend. She would not organize her weekend around it if they had. Just a woman who bought champagne and was drinking it on the way to wherever she was going, and cabinet reshuffles proceeding on a separate, parallel clock that occasionally intersects with hers as an air raid siren and otherwise does not.

There is a detail worth pausing on: the ambassadorial reshuffle in Washington seems to have driven the cabinet reshuffle in Kyiv — a diplomatic vacancy mattering more to Z than the head of government did. We read this as evidence of the government’s secondariness. It is better described as its whole biography. A government assembled by an office rather than a coalition was never going to behave otherwise. You cannot accuse a chair of failing to be a table.

The result is more foam — in Kyiv, at least. Ukraine has hit ninety Russian oil tankers in the Sea of Azov since July 6, and Russian milbloggers are doing something Kyiv’s commentariat rarely bothers with anymore: admitting, in public, that their system is broken, too.

The woman with the champagne, for what it’s worth, is hung over.
