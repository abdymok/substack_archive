*WSJ , August 15, 2026*

The genre trick here is simple: describe a woman who makes cruise missiles using the exact prose you’d use for a woman who makes throw pillows, and hope nobody notices the join. It almost works. Six days to two and a half hours — that’s the wing production time, and also roughly how long it takes the piece to go from “flower pots” to “2,500-pound warhead” without changing tone.

Pompeo shows up to vouch for her adaptability, because nothing says journalism like a character reference from a man whose own adaptability got him a board seat. The dog is named Chuck. The dog is always named something. It’s there so you’ll forgive the missile.

And then the koan: “in order to build something nice, first you have to destroy something ugly.” Print that on a mug. Better yet, print it on the missile — the Flamingo already comes in pink, might as well go all the way.

Then Nick [shows us](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/08/17/europe/ukraine-patriot-missile-launchers-run-dry-intl) what a Patriot missile launcher looks like sitting at the edge of a field in the middle of nowhere, beautifully photographed, entirely useless without the missiles.

There is something almost perfect about this. One story gives us the missile wrapped in flowers and carbon fiber; the other gives us the launcher sitting patiently in a field, waiting for its ammunition.

The flowers are apparently doing rather better.
