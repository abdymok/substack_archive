My old friend [Valentyn](https://knihi.com/Valancin_Taras/Na_vyspie_uspaminau_mp3.zip.html) knew nothing about physics but lots about tyrants, despots and their flunkies—the kind that entangled eastern Europe after the World War II—used to say: the information is in the correlations, not in the parts.

He meant this about people. John Preskill means it about the universe.

"\[The\] era of fault-tolerant quantum computing," [he writes](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.00862), "may still be rather distant. No one really knows how long it will take to get there."

A quantum system cannot be read sequentially. Page by page, there is nothing. The signal is in the relations.

[Image]

*Photo by abdymok enchanted with AI (March 24, 2026)*

Last month two men spent a day in Geneva moving between crises as though they were meetings. Before lunch: uranium. After lunch: Ukraine.

They were Trump’s son-in-law and golf partner[1](#footnote-1).

A third element has since been introduced.

Alexander Lukashenko—in office since 1994—[is being discussed](https://www.ft.com/content/69675c86-d1ff-4bbf-a55a-a480421bd165?syn-25a6b1a6=1) as a White House guest.

He released 250 prisoners. Then 500. An opening number, not a conclusion.

“Tell Donald Trump I agree to work out this big deal.”

No deal specified.

In quantum terms, a superposition: all outcomes held in reserve until observation collapses them.

Valentyn’s version was simpler. Despots and their auxiliaries form a system. You can adjust individual elements. The system remains.

Consider the pages together.

Iran: negotiations offered after bombing.
Ukraine: a settlement proposed without terms that hold.
Belarus: a meeting floated in exchange for the staged release of prisoners.

Individually, these are positions. Together, they describe a method.

In Moscow, Putin watches Tehran with interest. That could have been us.

He reads the system, not the page.

Valentyn understood this. The flunky manages the page. The tyrant reads the book.

Preskill, writing about qubits, notes that fault tolerance remains distant. The system cannot yet hold its state under stress.

He is describing a machine. The description applies.

Most Ukrainians do not expect a durable outcome. They are not participants in the design.

Valentyn would not have been surprised.

A sequence of pages produced without reference to what precedes them. No mechanism for reconciling the whole.

The structure persists.

The correlations, as always, outlast the parts.
