# Weakness Demands Annexation

*mysteries of an atom / looking into the abyss / seeing all the infinite fun*

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent explains that America needs Greenland because Europeans “project weakness.” Not minerals, not Arctic routes—weakness itself demands annexation. We’re the strongest country in the world, which settles matters. The dragon lived over the town for four hundred years, and they gave him maidens without so much as a meow. Good deeds you don’t forget—he boiled the lake once to prevent cholera[2](#footnote-2).

[Video: https://youtu.be/55FtfIk8U94]

Compare Trump’s Greenland script with Putin’s Ukraine justifications. Same text, different accent, identical grammar: The territory’s been weaponized by hostile forces. Both are historical accidents begging for correction. International law? Cobwebs. What matters is positioning justified by Pechenegs and Vikings. Occupation becomes response, refusal justifies force. Look what you made me do, now with tariffs.

*do you trust yourself, babe? / theory of random walks*

This is peace through strength: so peaceful it threatens military force against Denmark to acquire 57,000 people who’ve made clear they’re not interested.

What’s alarming isn’t one administration. It’s simultaneous emergence in multiple former superpowers. MAGA and the Russian World both dismiss universal values as liberal fairy tales, treat ultra-conservative principles as uniquely legitimate, view alternatives as threats requiring eradication, understand democracy as dictatorship of the clan, worship force itself.

Americans think they’re entering splendid isolation. They’re entering the world before 1945—multiple great powers, metastasizing conflict, no reliable friends. The American order is over not because the United States proved incapable, but because it decided it no longer wishes to. American might that upheld eighty years of order will now destroy it.

*diving into instinct / micro dosing ego death / soon it will be later*

What does Europe do facing hostile powers on eastern and western flanks? If Germany, Britain, France, Poland arm to full capacity—including nuclear weapons—they’ll deter Russia and make an American president think twice. A rearmed Germany without American guarantee will take nationalist views. France is one election from nationalist victory. Imagine rearmed nationalist France facing rearmed nationalist Germany. They fought three major wars in seventy years before the United States established peace.

Japanese rearmament heightens nervousness in South Korea, facing hostile nuclear North Korea and possibly nuclear Japan, which invaded Korea three times.

*& you need to show up babe / even if you feel like a storm*

China and Russia aren’t satiated status quo powers. They’re dissatisfied have-nots seeking to restore regional dominance. Russia’s only in early stages of rebuilding its sphere. Ukraine isn’t the end but the beginning. Xi speaks of “great changes unseen in a century.” For Putin, Trump’s destruction of the transatlantic alliance is such a change. Why shouldn’t he seize it?

The first years won’t be marked by adroit diplomacy but intense confrontation. More like the brutal multipolar early twentieth century than the orderly nineteenth.

*as I feel I’m watching you change / & I know I will do the same*

Bessent believes Europeans will “come around.” Serene confidence of someone who cannot imagine weaker parties might have interests differing from his assessment. The burgomaster kept saying: we’ve learned to understand each other from ancient times here. The entire town shall look at you with dread and rejoice at your downfall.

Trump’s managed in one year to destroy the order and weaken America’s ability to protect interests in what comes next. We built international order because we were strong enough to remember what unchecked strength produces. We’ve forgotten. The monsters wake, stretch, prepare to teach us again. The dragon crippled them exactly as required—souls with no hands, souls with no legs, mute souls, chained souls, dead souls.

*oh it’s another blessed day*
