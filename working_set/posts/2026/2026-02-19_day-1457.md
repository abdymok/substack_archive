You know how the intelligence analysts and geopolitical whiz kids have been bouncing around these ideas of hybrid warfare and fifth column infiltration: they write long threads about bad actors and institutional rot, about the structured paranoia of identifying traitors within one’s own ranks while scrolling through Telegram channels at 2am. Well of course it’s galling. It’s irritating that anyone anywhere can quantify/qualify the loyalty of a sitting general when it’s so freaking hard just to make up your mind about who’s actually running whom when presented with a cascade of burned documents, detained spooks, suspiciously timed explosions and backoffice shenanigans.

Even more galling is when it kind of lands. The allegations against Kyrylo Budanov — until recently head of Ukrainian military intelligence, now as of January 2nd 2026 Head of the Office of the President, a job that came with the previous occupant’s resignation still warm — teem with grey zones and burned files and classified materials that all interlock beautifully and make perfect sense to the upstart armchair analyst. Budanov, the claim goes, reportedly oversaw a procurement structure that failed to fulfill 83% of its arms contracts and left the army short of ammunition through 2022 and 2023.

Quite the résumé.

Most of this is, by design, impossible to verify. But one thing isn’t. Fourteen days after his appointment in August 2020, Budanov went on camera with a Putin flunkie employed by the President’s Office and said flatly that Wagnergate — the elaborate Ukrainian sting operation that lured 33 Wagner mercenaries to Belarus, where they were to board a flight to Istanbul that would have made a convenient emergency landing in Kyiv — was not a Ukrainian operation but an FSB-orchestrated one.

The [Bellingcat investigation](https://www.bellingcat.com/news/uk-and-europe/2021/11/17/inside-wagnergate-ukraines-brazen-sting-operation-to-snare-russian-mercenaries/), published fifteen months later and built on hundreds of verified documents, audio recordings, and interviews with participants on both sides, demolishes that claim with the kind of thoroughness usually reserved for war crimes tribunals. All of it Ukrainian, all of it documented. We covered Wagnergate in [September 2021](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/scale-free-wagner-pmc?utm_source=publication-search) and twice in November 2021 — [here](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/i-an-actor?utm_source=publication-search) and [here](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/pants-on-fire?utm_source=publication-search) — and have asked the woman claiming to be KonMarie running the backoffice of this Substack to make all of it available to non-paying subscribers, who should become founding members and paying subscribers anyway, for the hell of it.

Worth noting that the man Budanov replaced at the Office of the President is Andriy Yermak — the same Yermak who, per Bellingcat, requested the last-minute postponement that handed the Wagner mercenaries to Belarusian security services rather than Ukrainian ones, reportedly to protect a Donbas ceasefire announcement. One Wagnergate figure out, another in. The circle of life, Kyiv-style. And worth noting too that shortly after his appointment as chief of military intelligence, Budanov was found to be sharing a private estate in Khotiv, outside Kyiv, with the family of one [Oleksandr Hohilashvili](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/oleksandr-hohilashvili-busted) — a deputy interior minister whose story is best described as a greatest hits of the Z era: filmed berating police at a checkpoint in eastern Ukraine, reportedly a Russian citizen from Rostov with a criminal record, husband of Z’s personal aide and body woman Maria Levchenko, and a man whose appointment to shape state drug policy had been secured, apparently, by virtue of his wife’s proximity to the president. The estate itself belonged to the wife of Vyacheslav Strelkovsky, whom Ukrainian media identified as the organizer of large-scale cash conversion schemes. Budanov told Bihus.info he was only staying there temporarily while his apartment was being renovated. Naturally.

These are certainly not issues Piers Morgan asked Z about the other day during his 80-minute [uncensored interview](https://www.instagram.com/p/DU5w4CuCNS9/).

On the subject of elections, Z noted that when Russians tell Americans that Ukraine can hold elections in one or two weeks, “they always forget that we are not Russia.” — which is, all things considered, a stupid thing to say.

Meanwhile, in his wood-paneled office at the Ukrainian Embassy in London — walls hung with military aircraft posters and children’s drawings of battle scenes, toy drones on the mahogany table, live drone feeds from eastern Ukraine flickering on screens behind his desk, the whole aesthetic suggesting a man who has given the matter of his own legend considerable thought — Valeriy Zaluzhny is watching all of this from a careful distance. The general Z fired in February 2024 and packed off to Britain leads Z by three points in hypothetical presidential polling, turned down Paul Manafort who came calling in spring 2025, and says he’s not doing politics until the war is over. He also, in a [recent AP feature](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-war-zaluzhnyi-zelenskyy-raid-5ff9e5e2054e0b17c10daf58d7eab63e), described for the first time how dozens of SBU agents raided his command center in September 2022 — hours after a tense meeting at Z’s headquarters — ostensibly searching for a strip club that had closed at that address before the full-scale invasion even began.

That strip club, almost certainly, was Tootsie — a venue near and dear to this vlog’s heart, covered [here](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-469?utm_source=publication-search), [here](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/stay-out-of-doorways?utm_source=publication-search), [here](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/imposter-syndrome?utm_source=publication-search), and [here](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-767-59a?utm_source=publication-search), the last of which explores the dizzying connection between Wagnergate, Tootsie, and Ukraine’s subjectivity. Tootsie occupied the ground floor of Samsonov’s House, a Neo-Renaissance architectural monument at 30/10 Bohdan Khmelnytsky Street — built in 1900-1901, beloved of Kyiv’s early 20th century intelligentsia, damaged in the war, restored, and since 2000 popularly known as Klitschko’s House, its beneficial owner being one Volodymyr Klitschko, Ukrainian citizen registered in Monaco. The premises had housed, in sequence: a celebrated deli privatized after the Soviet collapse by the family of Vitali Klitschko’s godfather; a casino; and then, once gambling was banned, Tootsie — a strip club that became a favorite of Lev Parnas, former New York City mayor Rudy Giuliani, Hunter Biden, and this correspondent, back when Kyiv was Kyiv. In January 2022 police raided Tootsie in a human trafficking investigation and found an underground brothel, drugs, weapons, and evidence the club had been secretly filming visitors for blackmail purposes. Tootsie has reportedly since closed, its sign removed from the facade — though we have our doubts.

Zaluzhny called Yermak and told him he was prepared to call in military reinforcements to repel the search. The SBU says no search was carried out.

Kyrylo Budanov is currently a member of the Ukrainian delegation negotiating peace with Russia.
