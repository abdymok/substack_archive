Filming sex scenes this morning in the back of the Burger Bar’s mustard-yellow mid-1960s Chevrolet C10.

The truck did not ask for this. As I wrote that sentence the air alert sounded — ballistic missiles incoming — an object approaching that nobody can see, its nature announced only on arrival. This is also how we do astronomy.

Physicists have a result called the [no-hair theorem](https://www.cfa.harvard.edu/news/do-black-holes-have-hair?utm_source=chatgpt.com): whatever a black hole swallows, it forgets. Two numbers survive — mass, spin. Everything else is smoothed away at the horizon. In Kyiv we’ve known this for some time. We call it an investigation.

Take the operation to bail out Herman Halushchenko: twenty days, four bags, two hundred million hryvnias. A modest black hole, but dense enough for June.

The astrophysicists call it *matched filtering* — you compare the signal against a bank of known templates. We have our own bank. A minister is arrested; a bank goes briefly blind; a call is placed to someone with a good speakerphone voice; a hearing evaporates. We didn’t need [LIGO](https://ligo.org/). We had [NABU](https://nabu.gov.ua/) wiretaps — same principle, except the mirrors are hearts, and they don’t lie.

The financial-monitoring system is an event horizon in reverse, built to keep things from vanishing without trace. So it was placed into maintenance — an administrative singularity, invisible from outside, through which four bags of cash crossed into bail. What happens during maintenance is, by definition, causally disconnected from the rest of us. Not a metaphor. Architecture.

Physicists also worry about cosmic censorship — nature’s habit of wrapping her embarrassments in a horizon so no one sees a singularity spin indecently fast. Ours is a gentleman named Vova, surname untroubled by mention, the way one doesn’t name the landlord in a story about tenants.

And Z, informed after the fact of everything his own office ran, is an astronomer told that the object he’s been orbiting for years was a black hole all along. Hard to see the thing directly. You only get the shadow.

No matched filtering required. You can just look at it. The only fully transparent transaction inside that office for eight years — fittingly, the one with the smallest mass and the most spin.
