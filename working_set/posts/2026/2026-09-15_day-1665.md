Every bridegroom needs an uncle. Donald Trump Jr. found his in the Bahamas in May, in the person of Umar Kremlev — a man who did not exist, under that name, before 2010, and who has since acquired a boxing federation, a fortune from Gazprom, and a seat near Putin on flights to Beijing. Kremlev rented the island, paid for the fireworks, and asked, by his own account, for nothing.

[Image]

*Russian Businessman Paid for Donald Trump Jr.’s Lavish Wedding After-Party . The payments, totaling hundreds of thousands of dollars, represented an extraordinary flouting of ethical norms for an American president’s family member ( The New York Times , September 15, 2026)*

[ProPublica’s reporting](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin) puts the bill in the hundreds of thousands, routed through a Dubai entity the boxing association uses for its money. Trump Jr.’s spokesman didn’t dispute any of it — Kremlev, he said, is simply a friend, met through hunting, bonded over boxing, and never once, in all that friendship, has politics come up. The family’s fuller statement, once the story broke, was that friendship doesn’t require a political motive. It is the kind of sentence that asks to be believed rather than examined.

Which brings us, by the shortest possible road, to the friends closer to home.

Ukraine’s prosecutor general resigned last week, was suspended before the resignation could take effect, and is now said to be spending the week in Paris while parliament votes today on his removal. In the same stretch of days, the SBU was running its own operation against the head of the National Anti-Corruption Bureau. Z is nominally responsible for three of the country’s law-enforcement bodies, or five, or seven, depending on the count — and none of the counts currently inspire confidence.

[Image]

The timing is unkind. The war NATO is quietly banking on is a slow one: starve Russia of resources over years, not months, until it has no choice but a ceasefire. That bet assumes Ukraine’s own resources — political as much as material — hold up just as long. A prosecutor general fleeing the country mid-scandal is not the sort of thing that holds up.

Z, characteristically, is not selling that version of the war to his own public. He talks instead of peace measured in weeks, not years — forty days here, six months there — a country fed its ending in small installments rather than told the real one might be a long way off. There is no theory of epistemology that prioritizes truth over facts in ultra-competitive Ukraine-style politics.

Against this backdrop, the ceasefire theater has resumed on schedule. Trump [announced](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/117270009579288120) on Truth Social that Ukraine and Russia had each agreed to stop hitting the other’s energy infrastructure — an agreement that does not exist, between two parties who have agreed to nothing. Z’s reply did not correct the record so much as sidestep it:

Ukraine has offered partners to secure such agreement with Russia that will halt destruction of critical infrastructure.

Food, energy, and other elements of basic life guarantees must be protected, and Russian strikes are primarily directed against this. Every day, Ukrainian energy facilities, port infrastructure, logistics, even food and pharmaceutical warehouses become targets.

Ukraine is not sure that Russia has desire to comply with any agreement.

If partners are ready to secure Russia’s real willingness not to hit our electricity, our other energy facilities, critical infrastructure, and food transit, then we are, of course, ready to ensure corresponding absence of our strikes.

For this to be reliable, long-term, and have real effect on people’s lives, rather than collapsing after Russians hold their elections and receive temporary easing of tension regarding gasoline and diesel precisely for that period.

War must be ended. And de-escalation step regarding critical infrastructure can be first step toward peace.

We expect specifics from partners. - [Z Telegram](https://t.me/V_Zelenskiy_official/20871) (September 14, 2026)

Next week marks the seventh anniversary of their first meeting — and, fittingly, the two men may sit down together.
