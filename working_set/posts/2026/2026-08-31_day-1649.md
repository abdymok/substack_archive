Myla is 25 kilometers from Kyiv as the crow flies. On the horizon, along the highway to Zhytomyr, you can see what's left of the town, wrapped in black smoke. An arms depot exploded there, killing at least 38 people. Vyshneve — the town that was blown up in July — is only 12 kilometers from Kyiv, while the distance between Myla and Vyshneve is 24 kilometers.

More than one day’s worth of air alerts since Friday evening.

[Image]

Instead of enlisting in Ukraine’s military, former Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov is jetsetting around offering to help other countries build drone armies. He wants American investors to finance a private Karpish vehicle that will build and manage Ukrainian weapons companies.

[The Financial Times](https://archive.ph/SlZd8), which for years refused to acknowledge Russia had even invaded Ukraine, asked the obvious question: what, exactly, will Fedorov’s financial and managerial relationship with that vehicle be? The answer, so far, is missing.

The accommodating 1,350-word profile sets the terms of a comeback narrative that makes us all queasy.

A friend of mine described Mykhailo this way: an artisanal blend of Forrest Gump, Robert Kennedy Jr. without the brain damage, and Ostap Bender, with a dash of Capt. Renault.

We’ve been circling this hot mess for a while — see [Day 936](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-936), [Day 954](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-954), [Day 1421](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-1421), [Day 1542](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-1542), and [Day 1618](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-1618)[1](#footnote-1).

In other news, Kyrylo Budanov, head of the President’s Office, leaned into unity-and-tradition conservatism in a bizarre [90-minute TV interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKq9lkajnXc). The spymaster mystique is gone, replaced by bureaucratic reflex. Budanov entered public life via Wagnergate. He leaves the interview trailing Mindich, his career neatly bookended. No one takes him seriously.

It began with maps. In [Day 936 — “Sneakers on the ground”](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-936), the observation was almost banal: a missile does not fly on courage, it flies on terrain data, and that data — TERCOM’s contour maps — belongs to Americans, is licensed to allies at America’s pleasure. A small fact. Small facts are how empires remind you who owns the mountain.

By [Day 954 — “The AI War Lab”](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-954), the small fact had grown teeth. Alex Karp had been profiled, Ukraine had been called a “laboratory,” and I recall thinking: a laboratory is a fine thing to be, provided the lights stay on. I brought in Veblen’s leisure class here — not as decoration, but because there is something very precise about watching men who have never gone without heat theorize about resilience. An AI war lab, I wrote, is one bad winter away from being a very sophisticated ice cube.

[Day 1421 — “Meanwhile at Davos”](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-1421) filled in the origin myth, as these things always eventually get told at Davos, in a warm room, by a man named Mosley. Ten weeks after the invasion, Z had already invited Palantir’s chief to Kyiv and offered him the flattering fiction of “the Israel of Europe.” What Ukraine gave in return was not abstract — millions of hours of drone footage, a generation of engineers testing code against live death. This is the part the Davos telling always skips: who is the laboratory animal and who is the scientist depends entirely on which side of the invitation you’re standing.

By [Day 1542 — “The Delivery Mechanism”](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-1542), the argument had sharpened into something almost unkind, though I stand by it. Karp is received in the West the way one receives a generous uncle, not a defense contractor peddling a twenty-two-point manifesto about which civilizations are “dysfunctional.” And Fedorov — young, fluent, allergic to the word “ideology” — was the perfect envelope for that manifesto. He delivered Silicon Valley’s worldview into Kyiv labeled as innovation, tariff-free, no argument required, because who argues with a laptop.

Then he was sacked, and [Day 1618 — “Flebodia and Fedorov”](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-1618) recorded the exit interviews — tidy, reform-flavored, full of “wounded interests” and a system too corrupt to bear his transparency. What he did not mention, in any of those tidy sentences, was the Dynasty compound, or the court politics quietly eating the ministry from the inside. A man can tell you a true story and still leave out the load-bearing wall.

Which brings us, more or less, to a hotel room in Washington, where the same man is now asking Americans for money to build the next generation of the very thing he spent four years importing — this time, apparently, without the ministry, without the mandate, and without anyone in the room old enough to ask him what “new history of the war” is supposed to mean when the last chapter he wrote got him fired.
