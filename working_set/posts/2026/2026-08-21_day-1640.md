In news of the weird, Z imposed sanctions against the creators of the Russian animated series “Masha and the Bear.” The decree was published Thursday evening, August 20, [on the official website of the head of the Ukrainian state](https://www.president.gov.ua/news/ukrayina-zastosuvala-sankciyi-proti-pidpriyemstv-rosijskogo-105905). The restrictions apply to the “Animaccord” studio, which created the series, and five Russian citizens, including the studio’s founder and CEO, the show’s screenwriter, and the rights holder.

As explained on the Ukrainian president’s website, “Masha and the Bear” poses a threat to the country’s national security because it spreads “pro-Russian narratives disguised as children’s content.”
