*c/o Politico (May 20, 2026)*

There is something almost touching, in a metaphysical sense, about how the modern world has managed to conduct its disagreements. A Ukrainian drone, disoriented by Russian electronic wizardry, wanders into Estonian airspace like a confused guest at a party thrown by someone else. A Romanian fighter jet—stationed in Lithuania to prevent precisely this—must now apologize to its Ukrainian allies while simultaneously shooting them down. Everyone involved understands the script. Everyone insists they followed it correctly. This is what we call civilization.

I mention this because one of our founding members vacations in Latvia. He had planned to return next month. Now he finds himself in the peculiar position of hoping—sincerely—that Ukrainian drones remain sufficiently disoriented to avoid his favorite vaca hangouts, while simultaneously supporting Ukraine’s right to use any airspace necessary to strike Russian targets.

But there is more. [Z now warns](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/zelenskiy-says-russia-considering-plan-attack-nato-country-belarus-2026-05-15/) that Russia is considering plans to attack a NATO country *directly from Belarus*. So our founding member must now also hope that Russia does not successfully execute this plan, which would mean Article 5, which would mean NATO at war with Russia, which would mean Latvia is no longer merely a place where drones occasionally get lost but an actual theater of conventional warfare. The contradiction is no longer his alone. It is everyone’s.

Consider the architecture of the denials. [The Russians claim Ukraine plans to launch drones from Latvia.](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/1169055.html) [The Latvians deny this.](https://www.reuters.com/world/russia-warns-latvia-against-letting-ukraine-launch-drones-threatens-retaliation-2026-05-19/) [Ukraine apologizes while maintaining its right to strike targets in Russia.](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/may/19/russian-jamming-blamed-nato-jet-downs-ukrainian-drone-estonia) Each statement is technically true. Each is also beside the point.

What strikes the observer is not the chaos but its *courtesy*. Everyone apologizes. No one admits to wanting any of this. Our founding member checks airline schedules. In Minsk, Lukashenko [launches drills involving Russian nuclear weapons](https://www.dw.com/en/belarus-launches-drills-involving-russian-nuclear-weapons/a-77204498).
