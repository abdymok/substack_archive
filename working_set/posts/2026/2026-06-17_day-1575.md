Trump announces new sanctions on Russian oil, and the Europeans exhale as if a sentence had been commuted. They forget, briefly, that a sentence commuted by one man can be reinstated by the same man, usually before lunch.

The price of his affection is itemized like a menu: help clear the mines from Hormuz, applaud the Iran deal, and only then — perhaps — “some give on Ukraine.”

This is not an alliance. It’s a pawn shop with flags.

“Russia should make a deal” — this is what is said by people who have already decided, quietly, that no deal is required of them.

Z shows the table the burning cathedral a second time, this round aimed not at policy but at conscience — useful, since the policy was already spoken for.

November isn’t mentioned once [in the article](https://www.independent.ie/world-news/trump-says-g7-had-a-very-good-meeting-with-zelensky-at-summit/a/157328560.html)[1](#footnote-1). It doesn’t need to be. It arrives on schedule — the way winter always arrives on schedule.
