On the war jacket, absurdity as self-parody. Ukraine has spent years demonstrating that it can integrate, maintain, and fight with the Patriot system under the most demanding combat conditions on Earth. Yet when asked whether Kyiv could manufacture the interceptor missiles it already depends on, Trump’s answer was that the United States had to be “careful who we licence to.”

[Image]

*The Financial Times , July 31, 2026*

Careful not to license the country intercepting Russian ballistic missiles. One imagines Lockheed Martin can rest easy knowing the greater proliferation risk lies not in Moscow’s missile factories, but in the people trying to shoot their products down.

In related news, [The Guardian](http://theguardian.com) tells us that Russia [bombed](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jul/30/us-firm-drone-factory-terminal-autonomy-ukraine-russia) Terminal Autonomy on the night when male deer (bucks) were growing their new velvet-covered antlers.

Why would anyone in their right mind build a factory to produce drones in Ukraine?
