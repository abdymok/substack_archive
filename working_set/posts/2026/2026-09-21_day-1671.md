No transcript today. The video runs 12 seconds and ends with the last stanza of Miłosz’s [You Who Wronged](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/49482/you-who-wronged). The only sound — the approach of a Shahed drone and anti-aircraft fire — comes at the 12th second.

Not because it speaks for Ukrainians. Because it speaks for this project: write down the deed and the date, and let the record outlive the man.

One square, the 41st, is wired to appear 3% of the time. The rest, minus two, point to ukraine@war posts going back to 2019. The [40-square mural](https://abdymok.github.io/40/) is the thing itself.

Gemini picked the sample from 1,700 posts. It picked well enough. There is little time in Kyiv for curation.

The text is free, in the notebook, with no pictures. The pictures are the point, and they cost money.

We use pictures and sound as much as words. A century ago this year, Bernard Shaw refused his Nobel Prize money. This is no Project Gutenberg \#13635 — that was Shaw’s war, bound and numbered before anyone knew how it would end. Ours keeps recomposing.

His reason, in the form most often attributed to him — which happens to be French: *Le prix Nobel, c’est une bouée de sauvetage lancée à un nageur qui a déjà atteint la rive.* A lifebelt thrown to a swimmer who has already reached the shore.

He spoke the language badly.[¹](#user-content-fn-1) The line survives in French anyway — outliving him in a tongue he never quite had, the way a record is supposed to outlive the man who made it.

Words were never the thing.
