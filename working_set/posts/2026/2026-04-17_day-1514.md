We landed at No. 19, described as *“a long-running, intelligence-adjacent war diary (active since 2014).”*

Two problems with that. First: [Substack](http://substack.com) was founded in 2017, so the math doesn’t work. Second: *intelligence-adjacent* is doing a lot of heavy lifting for a publication whose most-read post — [Day 701:](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-701?utm_source=publication-search) *[Wartime Sketches](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-701?utm_source=publication-search)* — pulled in tens of thousands of readers thanks to a photograph of a nude woman studying herself in a mirror during a blackout winter in 2024.

The account posted Substack links. That’s it. That’s the whole account, which accounted for 29 viewers clicking on 298 pages daily.
