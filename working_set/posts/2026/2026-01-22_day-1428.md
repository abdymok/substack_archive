KonMari sent me a link to the speech by Canada’s PM Carney at Davos this year.

> V important speech, she said.

[Video: https://youtu.be/dTvFnC-oFGw]

My response:

> I remain underwhelmed. Better than Trump’s pathetic performance, sure. Revelatory? Only if you haven’t been paying attention.
>
> Mark invokes Havel’s greengrocer to position Canada as the brave truth-teller removing its sign from the window. But which sign is he actually removing?
>
> **The sign he claims to remove:** “The rules-based international order works”
>
> **The sign he’s placing:** “Canada leads a principled realignment of middle powers”
>
> Watch the sleight of hand. He condemns “living within a lie” while announcing strategic partnerships with China days before this speech, claiming Canada “applies the same standards to allies and rivals,” and rebranding transactional dealmaking as moral clarity.
>
> It’s a rehash of the framework we starting hashing out in November 2021[2](#footnote-2)[3](#footnote-3).
>
> **Proposition:** Canada’s “value-based realism” is just rebranded *realpolitik* that abandons liberal principles while maintaining their vocabulary.
>
> This is approaching **mutual knowledge** (diplomats know it, journalists carefully allude to it, opposition MPs grumble). But Mark’s speech tries to prevent it from becoming **common knowledge** by framing the abandonment of principles *as* a principled stance.
>
> “We are taking the sign out of the window” - while putting up a new sign that says the same thing in fancier language.
>
> The delusion is thinking that calling your transactional autocrat-friendly policy “variable geometry coalitions” makes it fundamentally different from what he’s criticizing. Those lips, that brain, that performance.
