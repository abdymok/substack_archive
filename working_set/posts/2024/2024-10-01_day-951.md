[Day of the Defender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defenders_Day_(Ukraine)). Everything came to an abrupt halt at 0900 this morning.

Z gave a [speech](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/vistup-prezidenta-z-nagodi-dnya-zahisnikiv-i-zahisnic-ukrayi-93597) at [Kyiv Fortress](https://www.google.com/maps/@50.4335778,30.534971,16z?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI0MDkyNS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), where police were out and about for hours beforehand. The president handed out a bunch of medals, some posthumously, including to members of the International Legion.

[Image]

Orderlies wheeled out patients from Ukraine’s Main Military Clinic Hospital next door. Cadets from military schools swore oaths online to protect the motherland, follow orders et cetera.

[Image]

The entire ceremony lasted about a half hour.
