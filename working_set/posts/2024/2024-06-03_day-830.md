Ukraine’s monopoly energy provider DTEK informs:

> On June 3, electricity consumption will be restricted throughout Ukraine from 00:00 to 24:00
>
> The limits allocated to our city will be enough for 80% of the needs. Therefore, stabilization outage schedules will be in effect throughout the day.
>
> We ask you to reduce the use of electricity to reduce the number and duration of blackouts. We thank everyone who heeds our advice.
>
> Light will definitely conquer the darkness! Believe in power engineers! - [DTEK](https://www.dtek-kem.com.ua/ua/news/3-chervnya-po-vsiy-teritoriji-ukrajini-z-00-00-do-24-00-diyatimut-obmezhennya-elektrospozhivannya)
