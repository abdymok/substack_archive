No good news to report from Ukraine, except maybe the weather and Easter.

[Alessio Mamo](https://www.instagram.com/alessio_mamo/?hl=en#) was in the neighborhood the other day, waiting for women to climb up steps from the Zverynetska metro. I would have taken him to see the spruced-up Acid Scout mural a block away, but I was downtown.

[Image]

Luke in his article, titled “[‘I love my country, but I can’t kill’: Ukrainian men evading conscription](https://www.theguardian.com/world/article/2024/may/04/i-love-my-country-but-i-cant-kill-ukrainian-men-evading-conscription). *As the war stretches on indefinitely, there are few eager recruits and Kyiv’s armed forces are short of soldiers*,” paints a bleak picture about the failed forced conscription campaign.

Men, in fact, are in abundance, but maybe not in these photos. I can only count three.

[Image]

[Image]

It’s hard to imagine why any military draft anywhere would be successful in the 21st century, unless, of course, your continued survival depends on it, which in Ukraine it definitely does. Z and his five or six top managers talk about the failed campaign rarely, preferring instead to complain about not receiving enough promised guns and ammo on time.
