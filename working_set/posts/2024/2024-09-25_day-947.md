There was something wrong with the end of yesterday’s post[1](#footnote-1). So I’ll start with a revision of it today.

Ukraine needs to get its own act together, a feat that requires the country’s leaders to be honest and competent.

Our mission in 2024 was to:

- unify the country,
- attract new recruits, motivate them,
- build excellent fall-back lines of defense,
- stop telling the world we are fighting for freedom and victory on behalf of western civilization.

We failed to achieve any of these goals, so we are stuck in the Plan B rut.
