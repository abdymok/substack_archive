[Sociological survey](https://kiis.com.ua/?lang=ukr&cat=reports&id=1421&page=1) results about arranging a ceasefire: Ukrainians are not opposed to talks, per se, but don’t want to make any territorial concessions. Some 44 percent of respondents in a ZN.UA Mirror [survey](https://zn.ua/ukr/UKRAINE/vijna-chi-mir-ukrajintsi-khochut-povernuti-kordoni-1991-roku-ale-chijimi-rukami-rezultati-sotsiolohichnoho-doslidzhennja.html) said it’s time to start such negotiations, compared to 35 percent who say they are not ready. More than 80 percent disagreed with any suggestion that Ukraine should cede any temporarily occupied territories (TOT) or additional territories to Russia for the sake of peace.

Russia currently occupies about 20% of Ukraine, including Crimea.
