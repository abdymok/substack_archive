[1](#footnote-anchor-1)

In [fluid dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_dynamics), a **Mach wave**, also known as a **weak discontinuity** is a pressure wave traveling with the [speed of sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_sound) caused by a slight change of [pressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure) added to a [compressible flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compressible_flow). These weak waves can combine in supersonic flow to become a [shock wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_wave) if sufficient Mach waves are present at any location.
