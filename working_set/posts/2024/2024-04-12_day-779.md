We have long passed out of the first stages of the war fever, in which men took up arms by instinct, by the desire to “live free or die,” not by pity excited by the labors of CNN’s Clarissa Ward, but by a clear sense of duty inculcated since the Orange Revolution, the Revolution of Dignity, the illegal annexation of Crimea and invasion of Donbas in 2014, the atrocities committed by invaders and so forth.

[Image]

The difficulty begins when all the men and women susceptible to this inducement have already volunteered, served and are worn out. Now we have to draw on the solid, sceptical, pragmatic residuum who know the value of their lives and services and liberties, and will not give them up except on substantial and honorable conditions.

These people know that it is one thing to fight for your country, and quite another to let the establishment enrich themselves in the rear. They also know that it is one thing to wipe out invaders and another to let that sacred mission be made an excuse for subjecting yourself to populist idiotarians. We cannot disregard the decision to ignore months of warnings of imminent invasion[1](#footnote-1), nor the wagnergate-cia op[2](#footnote-2), or the scandalous imprisonment of Roman Chervinsky on trumped up charges[3](#footnote-3).

The controversy between men of peace as to the merits, demerits, causes, and possible results of *total war* is becoming almost as dangerous and almost as noisy than the real conflict now being waged in Ukraine. The only difference between the two conflicts is that combatants on the zero line strive to kill the body, while populists aim to capitalize on emotions and annihilate common sense.

The business of the appeal to the nation for new recruits and international partners for enormous sums of money has failed to decide the question whether the slosh and tosh of bullshit that has been making us sick for months is accidental or deliberate.
