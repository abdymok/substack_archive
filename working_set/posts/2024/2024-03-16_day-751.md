The haircut cost UAH 250. Because of Iranian drone attacks, the barber was one hour late. I skipped the brow trimming part and paid UAH 300. We chatted about sleep habits, the International Legion and her friend from Sevastopol.

[Image]

The death count after two Russian tactical ballistic missile strikes in Odesa (a double-tap attack) rose to about 20 by the end of Friday. Some 70 people were injured. Pools of blood everywhere.

According to the AFU’s Southern Defense Forces, a three-story recreational building was destroyed by the strike, which also damaged other civilian infrastructure, including 10 houses and a gas pipeline. The government has declared March 16 a day of mourning for the victims.

The latest deadly attack was preventable, that is, if Team USA had not dithered. Speaker Mike has been delaying legislation to authorize supplemental military funding to Ukraine since, er, last fall[1](#footnote-1).
