Okay, I’ve had two dozen people point me to [this article](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/nord-stream-pipeline-explosion-real-story-da24839c) at [The Wall Street Journal](http://wsj.com), each one claiming disbelief that such a gathering of words could come into existence. We do now feel a need to say something. We feel a need to pile on. Because, for the first time in recent memory, the pile on bullshit reporting suddenly doesn’t feel deep enough.

The *[exclusive](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/nord-stream-pipeline-explosion-real-story-da24839c)* appearing on August 15 is very annoying, because extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence, and the author of the article, Bojan, provides none, zero, zilch[1](#footnote-1). Of course, neither did flaks at [The Washington Post](http://wapo.st) when Isabelle and David ran their *[exclusive](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2023/11/11/nordstream-bombing-ukraine-chervinsky/)* about same last November[2](#footnote-2).

At the time, Roman Chervinsky, in prison on trumped up charges, said this through his lawyer:

> Дивно, на які саме джерела посилаються [The Washington Post](http://wapo.st) і [Der Spiegel](http://spiegel.de), коли кажуть, що якісь джерела, якісь чиновники повідомили їм цю інформацію. Тому що якби таку операція справді було проведено, то [доступ] до цієї інформації був би дуже обмежений. Дуже обмежена кількість посадовців могла б про це знати.

That translates to:

> It is strange what sources [The Washington Post](http://wapo.st) and [Der Spiegel](http://spiegel.de) refer to when they say that *some sources, some officials* told them this information. Because if such an operation really was carried out, then [access] to this information would be very limited. A very limited number of officials would have known about it.

That Bojan mentions intelligence briefs circulated between the [MIVD](https://www.defensie.nl/onderwerpen/militaire-inlichtingen-en-veiligheid), CIA, Poles and Germans is unhelpful. Foreign intelligence agents in Kyiv are not highly regarded — indeed, they are derided and highly disregarded — for many reasons. Many are [Tootsie Club](https://en.tootsie.kiev.ua/) regulars, out of shape, mentally, physically and spiritually. They hang around with people like Rudy Guliani and are very slow runners.

**The question we want answered is this:**

*Why did a handful of senior editors at [The Washington Post](http://wapo.st),* [Der Spiegel](http://spiegel.de) and *[The Wall Street Journal](http://wsj.com)  decide to publish **the exact same bullshit story** about the destruction of twin natural-gas pipelines carrying billions of dollars worth of Russian gas to Europe?*
