Russia attacked again early this morning with more than two dozen X-101/X-555/X-55 cruise missiles, three Kalibr cruise missiles and 15 Shahed-type attack UAVs.

The Shahed-136/131 drones were launched from Chauda in Crimea, and X-101/X-555/X-55 cruise missiles from 10 Tu-95MS strategic bombers flying over the Caspian sea.

In addition, the Russians used four X-22 cruise missiles from Tu-22M3 bombers. They were launched from Sevastopol and Kursk: three Kalibr sea–based cruise missiles from carriers in the Black Sea near Novorossiysk and three Iskander-M ballistic missile from Cape Tarkhankut in Crimea and Russia’s Voronezh region. In addition, Russia fired five S-300 anti-aircraft guided missiles from Russia’s Belgorod region.

Most of the projectiles were shot down by Ukraine’s Air Force. As of this writing, at least three Kyiv residents died in the attack. The number is likely to rise.
