A bit [otherworldly](https://t.me/vanek_nikolaev) this morning. Ballistic and cruise missiles. Kyiv, Vasylkiv and Shostka (Sumy region). As of this writing, at least one Mig-31 remains airborne.

[Image]

In keeping with the sluggishness of 3G in Kyiv, a strange December 31 ish edition of [Axios](http://axios.com) just slowly spread across my screen, with a photo of Uncle Joe glad-handing Z at the NATO beano this July[1](#footnote-1).

It reminded me of yesterday’s similarly brisk little piece by [Caitlin Doornbos](https://nypost.com/author/caitlin-doornbos/), esteemed Washington correspondent for the [New York Post](http://nypost.com), that gawping, yappy butthole of a newspaper owned since 1993 by Rupert Murdoch, whose delusions of intellectual rigor and public bumbling provide reliable ongoing amusement[2](#footnote-2).

Like Rupert, Elon has done well this year[3](#footnote-3). Both masters of the universe preach that the market should decide everything. Maybe this year the market will decide something about them.

Consider it a New Year’s wish.

[Image]

The move from the old apartment ended yesterday, kind of. Last night I hung curtains, assembled partially the bed and a table. Missing one essential barrel nut! We’ll save the bookcase for this evening and uncork a bottle of Kindzmarauli to celebrate the end of 2024.

By February, at least, unpacking will be over.
