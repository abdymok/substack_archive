We all remember Bucha, yes? That was the scene of [a genocidal massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucha_massacre) at the start of total war in early 2022.

As of this writing, A total of 26 missiles of various types were launched:

- Twelve X-101/X-555/X-55 cruise missiles from T-95ms strategic aviation aircraft (Engels);
- Six Iskander-M/KN-23 ballistic missiles (Voronezh);
- Two Kalibr cruise missiles (Novorossiysk, Russia);
- Four X-59 guided missiles (TOT of Zaporizhia region, Kursk region - Russian Federation);
- Two S-300 anti-aircraft guided missiles (Belgorod region - Russian Federation).

💥 The Ukrainian Air Force and Defense Forces destroyed 13 missiles of various types:

- Eight X-101/X-555/X-55 cruise missiles;
- One Iskander-M/KN-23 ballistic missile;
- Two Kalibr cruise missiles;
- Two X-59 guided missiles.
