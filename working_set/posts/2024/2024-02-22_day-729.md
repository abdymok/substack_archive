Lately, I have been receiving lots of requests for tips about bicycling in and around Kyiv. The best advice I can give is **DON’T**, unless you have a black belt in a system of close combat, like ju-jitsu. Of course it’s fine if you have experience parkouring in big cities in northern America and western Europe, but the Golden Rule of “cyclists have the right of way, always” hasn’t exactly caught on in Kyiv. Yet. Because motorists here don’t always want to yield the right of way, so you have to think about avoiding right-of-way accidents always. Don’t bother with hand signals because many drivers think you are telling them to fuck off.
