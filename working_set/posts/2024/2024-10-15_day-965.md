As of early 2024, Ukraine's armed forces comprise approximately **75 regular brigades**, which include ground forces, marines, and airborne troops (about 700,000 soldiers). Additionally, there are around **31 brigades** within the Territorial Defense Forces. This brings the total estimated number of brigades in Ukraine to between **105 and 110**. However, some of these brigades may exist only in the imaginations of the country’s rulers or have limited operational capabilities due to the lack of equipment and/or shortage of new recruits.

The wording of the **Oath of Allegiance to the Ukrainian people** taken by Ukrainian soldiers is as follows:

> "I swear allegiance to the Ukrainian people, to defend Ukraine, its independence and territorial integrity, to obey the orders of my commanders and superiors, and to fulfill my military duty with honor and dignity."

There is no Ukrainian Uniform Code of Military Justice, per se, just chaos.

The [Oath of Enlistment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Armed_Forces_oath_of_enlistment) for the US military is as follows:

> "I, (state name of enlistee), do solemnly swear (or affirm) that I will support and defend the Constitution of the United States against all enemies, foreign and domestic; that I will bear true faith and allegiance to the same; and that I will obey the orders of the President of the United States and the orders of the officers appointed over me, according to regulations and the Uniform Code of Military Justice. (So help me God)."
