My destination yesterday was the lower lavra, No. 8, where a bunch of weird buildings popped up during the 00s under disgraced ex-President Yanukovych. He had planned to donate the entire compound to the Russian Orthodox Church (Moscow Patriarchate). The grounds belong to Ukrainian state, which is figuring out what to do with them.

Lots of talk about Plan A, Plan B, Plan C, etc. But there’s only one plan - survival.
