There are two ways of becoming mindless. One is when you think the same thing over and over again, so many times that you become sure it’s correct without thinking. The problem with this is that once you make a way of thinking second nature, you're never going to improve and circumstances are going to change. The last thing you want to be is mindless when your next-door neighbor tweaks and decides he wants to kill you.

Learning to be mindless on a single occasion is even more pernicious. That’s when your chief of staff tells you, for example, Russia won’t invade. You just accept it and you accept it because you're oblivious to future circumstances where you might need that information. When you process something mindlessly, it's not available anymore, it's not available for you to play with it and say, "Well, maybe this time, not under those circumstances." Et cetera.
