> **What is positive cognitive dissonance?** This is when Russia, being frankly weak economically, demographically and technologically, feels like an “empire on the rise” and actually (and not at all in words, not as a bluff) implements a large-scale plan for global expansion.
>
> **What is negative cognitive dissonance?** This is when the collective West, faced with global Russian expansion, does not consider Russia as a priority threat, because it cannot believe that this dying state, technologically backward and completely dependent on the export of raw materials, can have such serious ambitions.
>
> In the subconscious of Western elites, who have been observing the process of complex degradation of Russia for a quarter of a century, the opinion has been firmly imprinted that Russia is “playing tricks,” because most of what Russia is doing now no sane society will do otherwise than for fun.
>
> In principle, the message is correct. The catch is that Russia’s sanity is not an axiom, but a theorem that would be nice to prove. Perhaps Russia is even experiencing formational (civilizational) agony. This says a lot. The problem is that agony is precisely an extremely dangerous condition for others. It is capable of generating euphoria, which is quite enough for the last fatal bite.

An [observation](https://t.me/v_pastukhov/1150) by practicing philosopher and former Merck Sharp & Dohme legal adviser Volodymyr Pastukhov.
