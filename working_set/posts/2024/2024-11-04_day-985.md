Z and Tiger Team deserve one another, shame Ukraine must rely on them both.

More than 48 hours has passed since the drone encounter, so I can post the full video. I was standing on one of the highest points of Kyiv’s right bank looking east, almost directly at the Mother Ukraine statue, trying to take a panoramic photo of autumn foliage.

I thought about Tiger Team yesterday and re-read Chapter 8 of Alexander’s [War in Ukraine: Conflict, Strategy, and the Return of a Fractured World](https://muse.jhu.edu/book/122782).

> In devising our planning scenario, the Tiger Team got the scale, geography, and timing of Russia’s military operations almost exactly right. But we were wrong on almost everything else.

[Image]
