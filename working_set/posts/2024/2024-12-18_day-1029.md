[Michael](https://www.justsecurity.org/author/kelly/) and [Craig](https://www.justsecurity.org/author/martincraig/) wrote a long whatever for the [Just Security](http://justsecurity.org) blog, titled [Trump’s Endgame for the War in Ukraine](https://www.justsecurity.org/105451/trumps-endgame-ukraine-war/)[1](#footnote-1).

> At the end of the day, a Trump-Putin dialogue outlining the endgame of Russia’s war in Ukraine won’t be as much about Ukraine as President Z would have liked. It will be about Trump getting his second term off on what he perceives to be a strong start⎯settling the Ukraine war as promised, locking in cheap oil to offset the cost of his tariffs, securing foreign funding for our national security upgrades, and dampening the conflict in the Middle East.

This immediately reminded of the scene from the American sitcom Seinfeld.

[Image]

> Kramer: You know what the Ukraine is? It's a sitting duck. A road apple.
>
> Newman: The Ukraine is weak. It's feeble. I think it's time to put the hurt on the Ukraine.

We are in the 11th year of a war in which Ukrainians chose to resist invasion in the name of *freedom*, a concept too many people in America and Europe confuse with “getting away with stuff.”

[Eva](https://x.com/evahartog) has a big spread in [Politico](http://politico.eu) about the war that also made me queasy[2](#footnote-2).

> Ultimately, analysts, said, the outcome of the war will likely depend on the decisions of the combatants’ primary backers: NATO and China.

Actually, no. The outcome will depend, first and foremost, on Ukrainians. And it’s not “Three years in,” according to the subtitle of Eva’s essay, but “For more than a decade.”
