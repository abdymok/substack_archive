The last time I checked, the core principles of open society, to which I ascribe, remain unobjectionable.

1. **Individual Freedom**: Prioritize personal liberties and the rights of individuals.
2. **Humanitarian Ethics**: An open society is guided by ethical considerations that promote human welfare and dignity.
3. **Rational Criticism**: Open societies value critical thinking and rational discourse. They encourage questioning and debate over dogma, allowing for the evolution of ideas and policies based on evidence rather than ideology
4. **Pluralism**: A hallmark of open societies is the acceptance of diverse viewpoints and lifestyles.
5. **Democratic Governance**: Open societies advocate for democratic institutions that allow citizens to participate in decision-making processes. This includes fair elections, freedom of expression, and the rule of law
6. **Transparency and Accountability**: Governments in open societies are expected to operate transparently, with mechanisms in place to hold leaders accountable to the public.
7. **Access to Information**: Open societies emphasize the importance of access to information and knowledge as a means of empowering citizens. This means freedom of the press and the availability of diverse sources of information without censorship.
