Odesa native [Yury Khashchevatsky](https://uk.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A5%D0%B0%D1%89%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%86%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%AE%D1%80%D1%96%D0%B9_%D0%99%D0%BE%D1%81%D0%B8%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87) teamed up with [Peter Martsev](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9C%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%86%D0%B5%D0%B2,_%D0%9F%D1%91%D1%82%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87) in 1996 to make the above satirical documentary.
