The cheese metaphor is easy enough to grasp: Multiple layers of protection, imagined as cheese slices, block the spread of an unmitigated disaster. No one layer is perfect. Each has holes, and when the they align, *[fubar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FUBAR_(disambiguation)).* But several layers combined — equity, expression, justice — plus lots of violin music, education, art, culture, ibogaine, etc., in addition to basic governing competence — significantly reduces overall risk.

This explains why we should all agree to keep [Eric](https://carnegieendowment.org/experts/2254) as far away from Ukraine policy as possible. In addition, there is no compelling reason to believe anything [The Washington Post](http://wapo.st) says about managing the disaster response[1](#footnote-1).
