**Charismatic authority** is a form of leadership characterized by exceptional personal qualities and a strong appeal to followers, often seen in movements that arise in response to social or cultural disruptions[1](#footnote-1).

[Max Weber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Weber) defined it as: “resting on devotion to the exceptional sanctity, heroism or exemplary character of an individual person, and of the normative patterns or order revealed or ordained by him.” Charismatic rule is often found in authoritarian states, autocracies, dictatorships and theocracies.
