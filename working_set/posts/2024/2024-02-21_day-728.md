This morning, I read this: [Ukraine’s Fate Rests on Speaker Johnson](https://www.wsj.com/articles/ukraine-fate-rests-on-speaker-johnson-should-put-aid-up-for-avote-098ae2e7). *If he puts aid up for a vote, it would pass, but it would also threaten his position*.

Actually, no. We took the measure of Mike early, concluding that he wouldn’t be of much help[1](#footnote-1). Anyone who is preoccupied with the sexual orientation of others can’t be useful, especially if a supernatural dictator wakes him up a night, barking instructions[2](#footnote-2).

[Image]

Ukraine’s fate, as always, rests on the ability of Ukrainians to work around jackasses like Mike.
