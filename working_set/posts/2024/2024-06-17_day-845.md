In other news, practicing philosopher Volodymyr Pastukhov compares Russia to a [can of rotting tomatoes](https://t.me/v_pastukhov/1123), noting that it is almost impossible to predict when, exactly, it will explode. Under the German Nazi scenario, he writes, a little hole can made in the lid with a bayonet to release the pressure. When the smell of rotten eggs becomes too pungent, countries might join together and throw it into the dustbin of history.
