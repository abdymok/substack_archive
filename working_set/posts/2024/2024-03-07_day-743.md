A major European city, Odesa, was hit with a ballistic missile in broad daylight, after a 5-hour long major drone attack overnight, during the funeral of a mother and her 4-month month old baby, who were killed last week with four other children. One dozen people died in that attack.

[Video: https://youtu.be/J1S1N1mMihQ]

Z’s motorcade arrived in Odesa on Wednesday just before the latest strike, which killed five more people, for a meeting with Greek Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis, who was in the vicinity of the blast and had a great view of the “[mushroom cloud](https://edition.cnn.com/2024/03/06/europe/russia-missile-odesa-greece-russia-Z-intl/index.html)” (as filmed by a member of the Greek PM’s delegation on his smartphone below).

Ukraine's Defence Forces [have denied](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/03/6/7445297/) the strike on Odesa was planned to coincide with Z's visit, but nobody believes that.

Meanwhile, the number of people injured in the shelling of [Borova](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Borova,+Kharkiv+Oblast/@49.3074678,37.6221192,11.25z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x4120949c4232f2bd:0xf437087459b7eb99!8m2!3d49.3742511!4d37.6230139!16s%2Fg%2F120l35tt?entry=ttu) in Kharkiv region increased to seven. Among the victims are teenagers aged 12 to 16, most diagnosed with mild to moderate injuries, and a 24-year-old woman, a 73-year old man and a 44-year old woman. A 70-year old man died, according to [Kharkiv Regional Military Administration](https://t.me/kharkivoda/13444).
