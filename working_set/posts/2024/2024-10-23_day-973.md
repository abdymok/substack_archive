Our top cop, Prosecutor General Andriy Kostin, [resigned yesterday](https://www.politico.eu/article/ukraines-prosecutor-andriy-kostin-scandal-russia-troops/) after a meeting of the National Security and Defense Council dedicated to draft dodging and other shenanigans. Z later published a decree that will eliminate [Centers for Medical-Social Expertise (MSEC)](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/991586.html) under the Health Ministry responsible for certifying medical disabilities and issuing military waivers. The entire system is corrupt, almost as bad as the circus at [Territorial Centers of Recruitment and Social Support (TCC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territorial_Center_of_Recruitment_and_Social_Support) run by the Defefense Ministry.

[Image]

Turns out that abducting military eligible men nationwide and busing them to boot camps while simultaneously selling bogus medical deferments to public servants, mostly employees of law-enforcement agencies, is, er, **demotivational**. Last year, Z dismissed the heads of all TCCs in response to **similar corruption issues,** which still persist. Result: a gigantic mess, much worse than the [VA](https://www.va.gov/).
