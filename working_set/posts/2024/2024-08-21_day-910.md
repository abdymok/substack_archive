Ukraine’s High Anti-Corruption Court, however, has [postponed](https://suspilne.media/817707-golovi-antimonopolnogo-komitetu-kirilenku-ne-obrali-zapobiznij-zahid-sud-perenis-zasidanna-na-27-serpna/) ruling on his pre-trial confinement (bail or pre-trial confinement pending trial).

Construction of second- and third-line defensive fortifications is the responsibility of local authorities and the government, not the military.
