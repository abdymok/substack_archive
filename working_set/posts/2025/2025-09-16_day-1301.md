Team USA still hasn't figured out whether Russia deliberately sent 20 drones into Polish airspace on June 10. Polish air defense shot down four, which you'd think might clarify intent, but apparently not for the rocket scientists at NATO who have "no news to share."

Fortunately, Secretary Rubio cleared everything up [five days later](https://www.state.gov/releases/2025/09/secretary-of-state-marco-rubio-with-gillian-turner-of-fox-news). Those Russian drones violating NATO territory? Actually proof that Trump's peace efforts are working! "This is an example of why this war needs to end," Rubio explained, deploying the kind of logic that makes Alice in Wonderland seem like a documentary. Wars escalate, you see, so Russian weapons over Polish farms are basically a peace dividend.

The Secretary was particularly helpful noting these attacks "have no impact on the front lines" - surely comforting to Polish farmers wondering if the next drone might mistake their barn for a Ukrainian power plant. But hey, "Ukrainians are conducting strikes in Russia as well," so really, who's the aggressor here?

As if to validate Rubio's brilliant analysis, Russia demonstrated its commitment to ending the war the very next day - September 16th - by launching missiles at Zaporizhia that killed one person and injured 13, including two children. Three residential buildings caught fire, requiring 78 rescuers to mop up this latest example of peace progress. Russian forces also struck 12 settlements in Kharkiv region, killing a 62-year-old man and injuring five others, because nothing says "ceasefire negotiations" like targeting elderly civilians in their homes.

Meanwhile, the same think tank analysts who've been spectacularly wrong since day one are drafting reports about "escalation dynamics" while Trump posts Truth Social ultimatums about sanctions he'll never impose. "No one in the world has done more to end the Ukraine-Russia war than President Trump," Rubio assured us, presumably keeping a straight face.

The beautiful irony is that while Rubio explains Russian attacks as peace evidence and analysts theorize about strategic ambiguity, the actual answer is a simple 540-kilometer walk from Lviv to Kyiv. Those 14-17 days would teach more about the conflict than all their diplomatic spin and footnoted reports combined.

(***Above:** Ukrzaliznytsia Oleksandr Pertsovsky explains how Team Russia has ramped up attacks on the railway infrastructure, especially on hub stations*)

You'd see the bombed apartments that expose Rubio's "both sides" nonsense. You'd meet families whose reality makes a mockery of think tank predictions. You'd walk past the infrastructure those "harmless" drones target, making the strategic picture clear to anyone not paid to obscure it.

Polish farmers don't need Rubio explaining that Russian drones over their territory indicate peaceful intentions. The grandmother whose Zaporizhia building caught fire doesn't require briefings on escalation theory. The Polish air defense crews didn't wait for State Department assessments before shooting down those four drones.

But here we are: Russian missiles hit children, NATO officials shrug, and the Secretary of State explains it's all evidence that peace talks are progressing beautifully.

The superhero ballet dancer keeps getting squeezed, but don't worry - the giant hand is providing conflict resolution services.
