Over the weekend we asked ourselves if Donald’s special envoy to the Middle East and Russia was an aspirin tablet or a hockey puck. But we forgot to answer the question.

He obvi is a hockey puck, a pre-stoicism disciple [Charlie Croker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S9bF4X38O0o) type.

Steven, a Bronx native who migrated to Long Island, is not a *biscuit* in the put-down sense Don Rickles used the hard round disk[1](#footnote-1). He is an icon of Veblen’s version of *invidious distinction*. His father made ladies’ coats, his youngest son overdosed in California.
