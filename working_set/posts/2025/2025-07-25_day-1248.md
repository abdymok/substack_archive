**Duty of Care:**

I live near a hospital that opened a wing for wounded soldiers a few months ago. Military patients, many amputees, move through the parking lot in the early morning hours — some wheeling IV stands as they cross to the Fora grocery store.

This morning around 6 AM, I watched a middle-aged veteran with a mangled arm return to the ward after an apparent morning run. An experienced runner by his build — remarkably fit despite his injuries. He cursed quietly and asked the guards for directions to the nearest outdoor gym.

One might wonder: shouldn't medical staff be encouraging these men to rest, to sleep, to heal? Instead, they're out running the streets at dawn, trailing medical equipment behind them.
