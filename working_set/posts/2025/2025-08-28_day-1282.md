In other news, here’s a snappier version of practicing philosopher Volodymyr’s [Telegram post](https://t.me/v_pastukhov/1634) yesterday.

> **The Chemistry Problem**
>
> Trump and Putin developed solid chemistry in Alaska, but chemistry without action is just talk. Putin's demand for "voluntary surrender" turns their supposed friendship into something that stinks.
>
> Do they both get this? Absolutely. That's why there was no dinner—they lit the fire but never cooked the meal. Yet both keep playing at friendship, hoping to solve their problem at Ukraine's expense. Neither can afford to drop the act yet.
>
> Trump pauses, letting Putin "prove himself" by taking what he wants by force. Putin pauses, letting Trump pressure Z behind the scenes.
>
> **The Berserker Factor**
>
> Z looks like the main obstacle to their deal, but this misses the point. Z's will today is Ukraine's will—however controversial that sounds.
>
> This reminds me of a childhood book about berserkers—Vikings who fought in a frenzy, feeling no pain. That's Ukrainian society now. Not every individual Ukrainian, obviously, but as a collective political force: a berserker, full of rage. In this state, voluntary compromise is impossible.
>
> This is what everyone misses when discussing war or peace. Rational analysis fails because Ukrainian society isn't operating rationally and likely can't.
>
> When Trump tells Ukraine it has "bad cards," he doesn't grasp that Ukraine stopped looking at cards long ago. Americans base predictions on the possible—politics as the art of the possible. Ukrainians practice politics based on the impossible. In Ukraine today, expecting miracles is the basis for political decisions.
>
> **The Gun Problem**
>
> Trump and Putin understand each other perfectly. Their problem is that Ukrainians will only accept compromise under one condition: after the front has already collapsed catastrophically. The mere *threat* of collapse doesn't work when society expects miracles—no threat feels real.
>
> Peace won't come from Trump's "kind words" but from whether he'll put a gun to those words—whether he'll risk the ugly ultimatum of cutting off all Ukrainian support. Without that gun, Ukraine won't accept the deal. If Z suddenly changes course, I'll know the gun found his temple.
