Toretsk, smack dab in the middle of Donetsk region, was previously home to 30,000 people.

Since Donald took office, the war has become **more — not less** — deadly[1](#footnote-1). The rise in both civilian and military casualties in early 2025 marks a significant increase in the deadliness of the conflict compared to late 2024. The increase in civilian deaths is particularly notable, reversing a trend of lower casualties in late 2024 and attributed mainly to intensified missile attacks on urban centers. Military casualties for both sides remain at or above the highest rates observed during the war, with Russian invaders, in particular, sustaining record losses
