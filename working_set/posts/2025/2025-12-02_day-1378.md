In November, Ukraine shot down **9,707** air targets:

- 11 Kh-47 M2 Kinzhal aeroballistic missiles,
- 51 Kh-101 cruise missiles,
- 15 Iskander-M/KN-23 ballistic missiles,
- 20 Kalibr cruise missiles, 3 Kh-59/69 guided aircraft missiles,
- 19 Iskander-K cruise missiles,
- 2,939 Shahed attack UAVs, 361 reconnaissance UAVs,
- 6,288 UAVs of other types.

In October, Ukraine shot down **11,269** air targets:

- 6 Kh-47M2 Kinzhal aeroballistic missiles,
- 53 Kh-101 cruise missiles,
- 17 Iskander-M/KN-23 ballistic missiles,
- 13 Kalibr cruise missiles,
- 13 Kh-59/69 guided aircraft missiles,
- 30 Iskander-K cruise missiles,
- 2,416 Shahed attack UAVs,
- 591 reconnaissance UAVs
- 7,930 UAVs of other types.
