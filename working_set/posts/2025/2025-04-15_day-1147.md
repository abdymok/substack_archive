In News of the Weird, the [Silpo supermarket chain](https://silpo.ua/product/keks-velykodnii-verkhivka-979080) this year is selling the tops of Easter cakes separately. Easter is celebrated on Sunday, April 20.

Traditionalists and [Orthodox priests are not thrilled](https://suspilne.media/khmelnytskiy/992717-verhivki-pasok-vino-i-krasanki-hmelnickij-svasennik-poasniv-so-mozna-i-so-nebazano-osvacuvati-na-velikden/).

The first Easter cakes appeared during the Last Supper of Jesus Christ with the apostles, when they celebrated the Jewish Passover. Over time, the custom passed into the Christian tradition and became an integral part of the celebration of the Resurrection of Christ.

Traditionally, you bake your Easter cake, put it in a basket with a bunch of hard-boiled eggs, sausages, a bottle of wine and ask the priest to sprinkle holy water on it before taking it home and having breakfast.

Nowadays you can just buy what looks like a big sugary cookie.
