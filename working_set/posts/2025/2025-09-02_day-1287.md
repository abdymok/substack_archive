Listen to Ursula lugubriously lip up Europe's latest black eye in the [Der Spiegel](https://www.spiegel.de/ausland/ukraine-ursula-von-der-leyen-spricht-von-recht-praezisen-plaenen-fuer-eu-truppen-a-83a04cea-9679-4f67-b3b8-66bd3c7f6147) - "quite precise plans" (as opposed to "pretty precise" - [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/8ade14ca-7aa1-4413-887b-59712037665c)) for a multinational troop deployment to Ukraine, she says, with all the conviction of someone reading from a teleprompter while the building burns down behind her. The gist of the bit? A couple of wooly-headed Eurocrats responding to Donald's latest fit of foreign policy lunacy, dressed up as post-conflict security guarantees. That's it. Sustainable outcomes are now impossible. Typical prefab stroke material.

But Ursula takes it so seriously that she forgets there's ridicule already dripping from the confab (and that everyone in Washington's dank Trumpian circle-jerk has already [ranted about it](https://www.cnas.org/publications/podcast/ukraine-negotiations-confusing-activity-with-progress)) and puts on the tired old threadbare diplomatic gloves to dance like an idiot. "President Trump reassured us that there will be [an] American presence as part of the backstop," she bleats, as if Trump's word has ever been worth the hot air it takes to produce it. Might as well have simply stopped after saying 'Let's assume the president can put himself on the back burner for a while…'

Meanwhile, reality intrudes with its usual rudeness. Belaya Tserkva in Kyiv region was [hammered](https://www.pravda.com.ua/news/2025/09/2/7528890/) last night - because while Europe draws up its pretty precise plans, Putin draws blood. Russian drones knocked out power to 60,000 Ukrainians overnight, hitting Odesa and Chernihiv regions like clockwork. The Dragon keeps feeding while the knights debate seating arrangements at the round table.

The theater reaches peak absurdity when we learn that Vladimir and Xi are having [their own beano](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/after-talks-with-xi-modi-putin-says-nato-enlargement-has-be-addressed-ukraine-2025-09-01/) on the sidelines of some showpiece summit in China. Putin's sticking around for Wednesday's military parade - 80 years since WWII ended, and here we are again, watching Europe plan another intervention while dictators toast their partnership. "The longer the war drags on, the more Russia will need China's assistance," notes one analyst, as if this weren't blindingly obvious to anyone who's been awake since 2022.

Newly elected right-wing Polish President Karol Nawrocki [visits the White House tomorrow](https://bnn-news.com/polands-foreign-policy-from-regional-leader-to-setting-star-271454). Lots can go wrong. Nawrocki recently demanded, stupidly, reparations from Germany for World War II. Apparently, the war didn't start in 1939 and Russia owes nothing. This is the quality of leadership Europe's betting its "pretty precise plans" on - a man who thinks grievance theater from 1945 is a winning diplomatic strategy in 2025.

The Kremlin, naturally, claims that European powers are hindering Trump's peace efforts. Dmitry Peskov calls it the "European party of war" - which is rich, coming from the people who just spent another night dropping drones on Ukrainian power plants. "We are ready to resolve the problem by political and diplomatic means," says the spokesman for the regime that's been methodically destroying civilian infrastructure for three years running. "But so far we do not see reciprocity from Kyiv in this."

Reciprocity. As if Ukraine's failure to surrender fast enough is the real obstacle to peace.

And through it all, Z huddles with German lawmakers and vows retaliation with "[new deep strikes](https://t.me/V_Zelenskiy_official/15891)" planned inside Russia, while German Chancellor Friedrich Merz solemnly intones that the war could last "a long time" and won't end "at Ukraine's expense." Everyone positioning, everyone posturing, everyone pretending their latest statement matters more than the last.

The Dragon feeds. The knights plan. The maiden bleeds.
