# Thug Life

**November 2021**: Journalist Yuriy Butusov after Z’s five-hour presser: “I should have stood up and punched him in the face.” Only respect for the office held him back. We wrote then that Z had driven Ukraine into a double-tracked curve where the rear wheel bogey jumped the rails—flattening the economy while failing to prevent civilian deaths. Wagnergate, helicopter taxi services via the State Emergency Service, constant allegations of impropriety. “We must pull the lever,” Z said. But he was the trolley problem[1](#footnote-1).

[Image]

.**November 2025**: Z briefs US senators—Graham, Shaheen, Blumenthal, Klobuchar—on Pokrovsk, air defense, sanctions, abducted children. Credibility required.

Also Wednesday: His Kvartal 95 co-founder Tymur Mindich flees hours before investigators arrive with wiretaps documenting a $100 million nuclear kickback scheme. Thursday brings more exits: energy regulator Serhiy Pushkar slips out Tuesday night. Businessman Alexander Zuckerman vanishes.

They called it “slagbaum”—Dutch for “barrier.” Perfect: borrow a Low Countries word for your shakedown while begging Brussels for €140 billion in frozen Russian assets. Force Energoatom contractors to pay 10-15% or face blocked payments. Missiles destroyed the grid; insiders monetized the rubble.

Ukraine’s worst winter since tanks rolled toward Kyiv: February insolvency, Pokrovsk falling, 60% of gas capacity destroyed, plus “slagbaum.”

Investigators drop 1,000 hours of wiretaps as Brussels debates that reparations loan—blocked by Belgium and Slovakia. “Professor” (ex-energy minister Halushchenko), “Karlson” (Mindich), “Che Guevara” (ex-deputy PM Chernyshov, allegedly pocketing $500,000 via his wife).

Transcript errors? Irrelevant when shaking down nuclear contractors. Some recordings discuss delaying energy site fortifications for better margins. Why protect infrastructure from missiles when there’s money to extract?

Z, elected to end corruption, watches agencies he tried gutting last summer expose his circle. Two ministers fired. Sanctions called for against Mindich—already abroad. UAH 95 million bail for money launderers still in custody.

Wednesday’s Yermak-EU Ambassador Maternova meeting: damage control theater. She “noted” Z’s statements. Brussels calculates: keep funding a government whose insiders grifted millions from the nuclear sector?

Ukrainian adviser’s summary: “This is ammunition for those... to say, ‘Why are we doing this?’”

The trolley jumped the rails years ago. Z’s own people saying it means we’re screwed.
