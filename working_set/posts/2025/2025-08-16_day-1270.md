I took the first metro to nowhere very, very early. Most of my fellow passengers were only half awake.

Three days running (Days 1267, 1268, 1269) I thought about the impending disaster. But even my most cynical predictions couldn't capture the spectacular awfulness that had just emanated from Anchorage.

Three hours of "extremely productive" talks produced zero deals, zero Russian concessions, and one grinning war criminal getting the full presidential treatment. Putin got everything: American legitimacy, global stage, zero accountability, and a joy ride in "the Beast." Trump got to clap enthusiastically while Putin approached down the red carpet like some demented awards ceremony.

Picture this: B-2 bombers—aircraft designed to vaporize Russia—flying overhead while Trump gives Putin friendly arm taps and lets him cruise shotgun in the presidential limo. Putin was "laughing with glee" inside the armored vehicle meant to protect American presidents from threats exactly like Putin.

When reporters shouted "President Putin, will you stop killing civilians?" Putin just shrugged. Classic Vladimir. Meanwhile, Trump—the man who promised to end this war in 24 hours—stood grinning under a "Pursuing Peace" banner.

[The actual transcript](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/transcript-of-what-putin-trump-said-in-alaska/) is even worse than the reporting. Trump's rambling: "We had an extremely productive meeting, and many points were agreed to... One is probably the most significant, but we have a very good chance of getting there. We didn't get there." Translation: We agreed on nothing important.

Putin delivered a masterclass in manipulation, praising Trump's "precious" efforts to understand "this history" while positioning Russia as Ukraine's hurt older brother. He got Trump to validate his greatest hit: "Today, when President Trump is saying that if he was the president back then, there would be no war... I can confirm that." Then came the pièce de résistance—Putin suggesting "Next time in Moscow" and Trump responding "I could see it possibly happening."

Here's where farce meets tragedy: Trump immediately threw the ball to Z. "Now it's really up to President Z to get it done." The master dealmaker just outsourced the job to the guy he previously shook down in the Oval Office. Z is flying to Washington Monday to discuss Trump's botched adventure.

Putin walked away with American validation and a U.S. president declaring Ukraine must now "get it done." His demands remain unchanged—denazification, demilitarization, territorial surrender. Trump walked away with vibes and a "fantastic relationship with Vladimir."

Meanwhile, Z gets to explain to dying Ukrainians why their American patron just gave their invader a legitimacy bath, then commanded them to negotiate directly with their tormentor.

This wasn't Munich 1938—that required actual negotiation. This was watching someone invite the neighborhood psychopath over for dinner, let him trash the place, then announce the victims need to work things out.

We're watching the sequel to Brest-Litovsk, except this time the Americans are helping write the surrender terms. The war grinds on. People die. And Putin just got everything he wanted: respect, recognition, and a red carpet rolled out by the leader of the free world.

Three hours. Zero deals. One diplomatic catastrophe masquerading as "great progress." Welcome to American foreign policy in 2025: making the world safe for authoritarianism, one summit at a time.
