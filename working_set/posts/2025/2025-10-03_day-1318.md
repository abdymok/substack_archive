Today is Friday, October 3—Day 1318. Of this I am certain. It’s about 3 a.m and Lubny (population 50,000), a city 200 kilometers east of Kyiv, is under attack. Cruise missiles and drones, according to the map above.

It’s still very early and a lot can change.

Lubny is one of the oldest cities in Ukraine.

- It’s a historic city but not a major railway junction
- No major power-generating facilities
- No publicly documented military bases

You pass through the city on the M03 highway driving to Kyiv from Kharkiv. There is a photograph floating around somewhere taken in 2006 of me standing in front of a moose statue in Lubny during the winter holding what looks to be a hash pipe. Hungry stray dogs are also in the frame.
