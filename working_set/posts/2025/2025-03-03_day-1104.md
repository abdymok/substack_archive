On March 1, [Russia killed and wounded more than 100 recruits](https://t.me/ssternenko/40762) of what remained of the newly formed 157th Mechanized Brigade in Cherskaske, Dnipropetrovsk region with an Iskander-M strike. A reconnaissance drone more than 100 kilometers inside Ukrainian territory was used to guide the short-range (500 kilometer) ballistic missile, which obliterated a large tent encampment in the middle of nowhere.

The 157th, like other brigades in the 150th series, had been slapped together hastily without proper training, sent to Pokrovsk and then cannibalized to replenish other brigades fighting in eastern Ukraine.

More than 100,000 soldiers have been charged under Ukraine’s desertion laws since Russia invaded in February 2022, according to the country’s General Prosecutor’s Office.

Nearly half have gone AWOL in 2024 after Kyiv launched an [poorly managed and controversial mobilization drive](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-conscription-law-a8ccc48e6c15276c5b5035288ed016ee) that government officials and military commanders now admit was a failure. .

There were an estimated 300,000 Ukrainian soldiers engaged in combat before the mobilization drive began. And the actual number of soldiers absent without leave may be much higher. One lawmaker with knowledge of military matters estimated [told the Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/deserters-awol-ukraine-russia-war-def676562552d42bc5d593363c9e5ea0) that it could be as high as 200,000.
