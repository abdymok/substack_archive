As of this writing, [at least 10 people are dead and scores wounded](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/russias-attack-kyiv-region-kills-one-sparks-fires-ukraine-says-2025-06-23/) in Russia’s latest shelling of Kyiv after a ballistic missile smashed into a residential building in the capital’s Shevchenkivsky district. Other parts of the city were also damaged by Shahed drones, including the swimming pool of Kyiv Polytechnical Institute (below).

The air alert lasted from around midnight to 3.30, and then again from 3.45 to 4.30. Ukraine’s Airforce said it shot down 339 out of 352 Shahed-type strike UAVs and simulators, **10 out of 11 Iskander-M/KN-23 ballistic missiles** and five out of five Iskander-K cruise missiles.

Direct hits were recorded at six locations, as well as falling debris at 25 locations in Kyiv and environs.
