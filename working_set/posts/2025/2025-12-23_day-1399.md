**Meanwhile, at the [Institute for the Study of War](https://understandingwar.org/research/russia-ukraine/russian-offensive-campaign-assessment-december-22-2025/)**

Putin’s getting “rosy” battlefield reports from his generals, who’ve apparently decided that honesty is overrated when briefing an autocrat. Chief of the General Staff Gerasimov is painting such optimistic pictures that Putin believes Russia can simply outlast Ukraine—never mind that Russian officials admit 2026 will be the “first difficult year” and whisper about potential banking crises. The culture of lying runs so deep that Russian milbloggers complain about commanders submitting “beautiful” but false reports that get their own troops killed. Putin, believing his own propaganda and the inflated reports, keeps rejecting peace proposals and insisting Russia wants all of Donetsk Oblast—something that would take years to achieve militarily, assuming Russia could manage it at all. Russia rejected the US-proposed Christmas ceasefire and continues demanding Ukraine withdraw from territories it hasn’t even occupied yet. Ryabkov promises Russia won’t attack NATO or EU states, which is about as reassuring as the 1994 Budapest Memorandum turned out to be.
