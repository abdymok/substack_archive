[DeepState](https://deepstatemap.live/#12/48.2992971/37.2079468) says the situation in the Pokrovsk-Myrhohrad agglomeration is sad.

[Image]

The area shown on this map slice covers approximately 400 square kilometers. There is no electricity, no centralized heating, no running water. Thousands of soldiers move through the ruins, fighting for survival. Ukraine’s logistics infrastructure in and around the agglomeration was obliterated in July, making entry and exit nearly impossible.

To put it in perspective, 400 km² ≈ 11–13 % of Long Island — about the land area from, say, Hempstead to Massapequa or Westhampton to Montauk.
