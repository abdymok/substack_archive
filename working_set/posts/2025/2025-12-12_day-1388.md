No one has dared show ordinary Ukrainians Trump’s twenty-point plan.

Z says the next steps involve chopping each point into more detailed documents—classic bureaucratic delay disguised as progress. The sensitive issue? Territory. Russia wants all of Donbas. Ukraine cannot accept that. The Americans float concepts like “free economic zone” while Russians call it “demilitarized zone.” Z’s position: stand where we stand, on the contact line. Three incompatible visions pretending to negotiate.

Europe’s strategy? Force Trump to smother Ukraine under klieg lights rather than in darkness. The theory being that Trump, confronted with his conscience, will swerve at the last moment. The problem is that Trump may not swerve. All evidence suggests he’s accelerating toward the crash.

> As for the 20 points, there are some positions that still raise questions. In particular, the issue of territory is very sensitive. The Russians want the whole of Donbas, but we obviously cannot accept that. The Americans are looking for some kind of format. They discussed the issue of a ‘free economic zone’. The Americans call it that, while the Russians call it a ‘demilitarised zone’. Our position is that it is fair to stand where we stand, i.e. on the contact line. There is currently a discussion between these positions,” — [Z](https://babel.ua/texts/123664-yakshcho-rosiyani-zalishat-sobi-zaes-vona-ne-bude-pracyuvati-pro-shcho-prezident-rozkazav-zhurnalistam-na-ne-zovsim-zakritiy-zustrichi) (*[Paraphrased by Babel](https://babel.ua/texts/123664-yakshcho-rosiyani-zalishat-sobi-zaes-vona-ne-bude-pracyuvati-pro-shcho-prezident-rozkazav-zhurnalistam-na-ne-zovsim-zakritiy-zustrichi)*, December 12, 2025).
