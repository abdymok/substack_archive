We left a blank yesterday for Z to explain how he plans to handle the golden toilet mafia scandal—Operation Midas, featuring over 1,000 wiretaps spanning 15 months, the equivalent of 94 *War and Peace* novels documenting back-office corruption. Instead, he told his parliamentary faction: “Everyone should work for Ukraine, that’s how it will be.” Translation: I intend to continue running this country like Slobodan Milošević.

The atmosphere in Kyiv increasingly resembles Belgrade in late 1998—alternative music, biting satire, political art, social life as coping mechanism, cultural resilience masking exhaustion. A city sensing catastrophe, defiance mixing with dread.

Enter Trump’s “peace plan,” timing so exquisite it might be satire. After decades posturing as America’s toughest dealmaker, he’s revealed his diplomatic genius: capitulation rebranded as statesmanship. The 28-point proposal isn’t negotiation—it’s Putin’s maximalist 2022 Istanbul demands with an American stamp.

> [US] officials said they received “positive feedback” from Ukraine’s Secretary of the National Security and Defense Council Rustem Umerov during discussions with Witkoff in Miami in recent weeks, but stopped short of saying Kyiv signed off on the deal.

> “The plan was drawn up immediately following discussions with one of the most senior members of Z’s administration, Umerov,” one of the officials said. “So Umerov agreed to the majority of this plan, and he made several modifications to it, which we included and presented it to Z - [The New York Post](https://nypost.com/2025/11/20/us-news/senior-us-officials-confirm-details-of-28-point-plan-to-end-ukraine-war/) (November 21, 2025)

Umerov is Ukraine’s former minister of defense implicated in shady real estate deals in Boca Raton, et cetera. How those UAH 23 billion ($550 million) were divvied up [last year](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/npas/pytannia-delehuvannia-povnovazhen-dlia-zdiisnennia-oboronnykh-zakupiv-1191r) remains a mystery.

> At the core of the deal lies the partition of Ukraine – to be recognised by everyone except Ukraine itself (they won’t subject it to further humiliation). The partition is harsh, but at the same time modest if you compare it to the original ambitions: Crimea, two practically destroyed regions, plus a small extra strip in the South, all paid for with at least half a million lives and an incalculable amount of money. Not exactly a massive prize, frankly. “The world’s second army” failed, in four years, to achieve what Manstein or Rokossovsky managed in a matter of months (back and forth). All in all, it’s a failure whose consequences will only be fully grasped later, in all their terrible magnitude. But at first, of course, it will be sold as a colossal victory. - [Practicing philosopher Volodymyr Pastukhov](https://t.me/pastukhov_eng/45) (November 19, 2025)

The arithmetic of humiliation is simple: Ukraine surrenders 20% of its territory, slashes its military in half, constitutionally renounces NATO, and trusts “security guarantees” from a president whose promises expire faster than milk. Russia’s concessions? Precisely zero. Putin receives sanctions relief, G8 reinstatement, joint investment vehicles with Washington, and 50% profits from his own frozen assets.

[The Institute for the Study of War](https://understandingwar.org/research/russia-ukraine/russian-offensive-campaign-assessment-november-20-2025/) spells out what Trump cannot grasp: forcing Ukrainian withdrawal from Donetsk creates indefensible lines favoring Russian reattack. The Kremlin explicitly states it will achieve war goals “diplomatically or militarily”—this “peace” merely lets Russia rest, reconstitute, and return against a neutered, territorially amputated Ukraine.

Most obscene is the process: developed with Moscow, imposed on Kyiv without consultation, revealed to allies through leaks. Trump promised to stop Putin’s war in 24 hours. A year later, he’s delivered Ukraine gift-wrapped to subjugation while Z channels Milošević and the golden toilets flush.
