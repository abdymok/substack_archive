Donald mentioned Ukraine [13 times](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-remarks-cabinet-meeting-white-house-august-26-2025/) during his marathon cabinet meeting on Thursday[1](#footnote-1). He didn't say anything new.

Since his return to the White House, Team USA has imposed **zero penalties** on Russia for its genocidal war against Ukraine, now in its 12th year. Instead, Donald has interrupted arms deliveries, cancelled assistance programs, ended international isolation of Putin and repeated Kremlin talking points.

The reason Team USA doesn't have an ambassador to Ukraine? The old one resigned rather than participate in appeasing the aggressor.

In Donald's fluid understanding of military ratios, Russia keeps growing. On August 26, he claimed Ukraine was fighting someone "[15 times your size.](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-remarks-cabinet-meeting-white-house-august-26-2025/)" Just six days earlier on Fox & Friends, Russia was only "[10 times your size.](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/trump-blames-ukraine-taking-nation-132221123.html)" Apparently Russia gained 50% more mass in less than a week. At this rate, Russia will be roughly the size of Jupiter by Christmas.

The get-together was pure performance art. As the New York Times observed[2](#footnote-2), it resembled reality television more than governance, with Donald demanding praise while cameras rolled. Ukraine was just one item on his "ever-growing list" of pet peeves—sandwiched between complaints about windmills, traffic medians, and water pressure.

The star performer was Steve Witkoff, Donald's "peace envoy" and golf buddy, who gushed that Donald deserved the Nobel Peace Prize as "the single finest candidate since the Nobel Peace...this Noble Award was ever talked about." **Peace through strength really, really works**, Witkoff declared

[Image]

.

This is the same biscuit who, months earlier, [demonstrated](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-1123?utm_source=publication-search) his profound expertise on Tucker Carlson. He couldn't name all four Ukrainian regions Russia annexed, stumbling through "Donbas, Crimea... uh... Luhansk... uh... and there are two others." He falsely claimed Ukrainian forces were "surrounded" in Kursk when intelligence showed they weren't. Most impressively, he parroted Putin's propaganda about sham "referendums" showing residents wanted Russian rule—when no legitimate self-determination has ever occurred in any part of Ukraine.

But don't worry—Witkoff has repeatedly assured Donald he's "the only person who could solve the Russia-Ukraine war." Donald added with characteristic false modesty: "I have no ego when it comes to this stuff."

**Peace through stupidity really, really does not work.** Team USA's appeasement strategy has only made the war longer and more deadly. Case in point: immediately after Donald got off the phone with Putin recently, swarms of Iranian Shahed drones descended on Kyiv, followed by North Korean ballistic missiles. Because nothing says "peace through strength" like your negotiating partner bombing your ally while you're supposedly negotiating.

As I gazed across the Dnipro after that morning's bombardment, the disconnect was crystal clear: Donald is obvi Putin's chump.

To quote a former heat-seeking douche rocket for Donald’s ass:

> If America presides over negotiations that result in a win for Putin, we won’t be ending this war — we’ll be guaranteeing the next one[3](#footnote-3) - Former US Secretary of State Mike Pompeo

But why listen to Mike when you have a real estate mogul who can't locate Zaporizhia on a map negotiating the fate of 44 million Ukrainians?[4](#footnote-4)

Unfortunately, surrender isn't an option—that would mean death or exile. So here we are: a Nobel Peace Prize nominee whose "peace through strength" consists of mathematical confusion, geographical ignorance, and immediate post-negotiation bombings. But at least the Nobel committee knows where to send the participation trophy.
