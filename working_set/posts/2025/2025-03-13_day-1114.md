The key difference between **truce** (*перемир'я*) and **ceasefire** (*припинення вогню*) lies in their binding nature. In English, a **truce** is generally informal and non-binding, representing a simple halt in fighting, while a **ceasefire** is typically meant to be binding. In Ukrainian, the converse is true, literally.
