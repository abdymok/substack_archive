Setting aside Donald's perpetual idiocracy, the Steven-Vova summit produced one concrete result: hints of a potential Donald-Vova tête-à-tête. Two theories emerge: either Steven delivered an irresistible offer (possibly unrelated to Ukraine), or Vova threw Donald a diplomatic lifeline—an "air truce" to save face.

The real question: will Donald settle for a consolation prize?

This brewing bilateral reads like a Neverland encounter. The casting is obvious—one player auditioned for the eternal boy promising to vanish problems with magic dust. The other? Captain Hook, complete with his own fleet and a keen interest in keeping Ukraine under his hook — permanently.

Meanwhile, the Lost Boys — Z and the leaders of Germany, France, Britain, and Italy — huddle in Zoom chats, waiting for Peter Pan to return with grand promises. They've been holding the fort against Hook's pirates, but their slingshots are running low on ammunition, and Peter's been trying to create his own truth, often by repeating the same bullshit over and over again.

But fairy tales have inconvenient endings. Peter Pan keeps returning to the same window, convinced this time will be different. Meanwhile, Hook has played this game longer and knows exactly where the crocodile lurks. The Lost Boys? They're starting to wonder if Peter might just fly away and leave them to face Hook's cannons alone.

Will pixie dust defeat cunning? Or will someone discover that happy thoughts don’t end wars?
