> I will try to say this with as little emotion as possible:
>
> If no one signs an order to withdraw troops from Pokrovsk and Myrnohrad in the near future, we may find ourselves in a situation where we not only lose a significant number of highly motivated paratroopers and marines (not to mention hundreds of millions in property, which is already lost and cannot be evacuated), but we may also find ourselves in a situation where there is no one to patch up the hole in the front line, and the fortifications we have dug up in the rear will quickly fall into enemy hands.
>
> The General Staff’s reports to the top are becoming more and more full of lies every day. We have already practically lost Pokrovsk, which means that there is no point in holding Myrnohrad either (logistics will remain only with inadequate risk and fields). And I am not revealing any great secret here; the Russians can see this perfectly well from their drones in real time. ￼￼This must be acknowledged, and those who refuse to leave without orders must be rescued. There is no need to fear a drop in ratings, because there will be no elections: next year there will be war again. And someone will have to fight it.— Vitaliy Denega ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/story.php?story_fbid=25876487601940569&id=100000180490959&mibextid=wwXIfr&rdid=KPGC4MaLLIfWwe6t#), November 2, 2025

The civilian population of the Pokrovsky-Myrnohrad agglomeration is about 100,000 people. Many have evacuated.

Yuriy was also skeptical about Friday’s helicopter assault by special forces near Pokrovsk.

> **An illiterate tactical decision**
>
> I saw a wave of positive reviews on social networks, when one general sent a landing party in the form of two detachments of fighters to the outskirts of a semi-encircled city.
>
> In fact, in a tactical plan in a modern war, the landing of two detachments of fighters in an open place in a kill zone, in full view of enemy drones, is an illiterate tactical decision.
>
> First, in a modern war, overcoming a kill zone is possible only if the maneuver is hidden from drone observation, the enemy notices any demonstrative actions and then everything flies at the fighters and their shelter, including guided bombs.
>
> Secondly, two detachments of special forces cannot significantly affect the situation in a city for which several brigades and regiments are fighting. And where the main problem is to provide logistics and maneuver to those who are already there.
>
> Thirdly, a precedent is being created once again when illiterate tactical actions, instead of an objective assessment and after action review, receive a lot of praise from bloggers, mistakes are covered up with PR, and therefore groups continue to be sent on high-profile heroic missions, the losses of which are never disclosed. Just as the real results are not announced. Because otherwise the assessments of such actions would be completely different. - Yuriy Butusov ([Censor.net](https://censor.net/ru/blogs/3582870/visadka-b-yits-v-na-vidu-u-vorojih-dron-v-tse-bezgramotne-taktichne-r-shennya), November 2, 2025)
