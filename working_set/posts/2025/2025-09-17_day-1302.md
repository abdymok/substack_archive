Amid all the heartbreak and destruction, these two op-eds reveal strategic confusion over Ukraine, though the authors seem blissfully unaware of it.

**Foreign Affairs** serves up the [more sophisticated confusion](https://www.foreignaffairs.com/russia/europes-delayed-reckoning-russia), correctly diagnosing Europe's predicament—caught between "a revisionist Kremlin and an indifferent White House." The brilliant solution? Europe should embrace Putin's own subversion tactics while simultaneously keeping diplomatic channels open for post-Putin reconciliation. It's the foreign policy equivalent of playing good cop and bad cop with yourself[1](#footnote-1).

**Friedman** delivers [pure therapeutic venting](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/09/16/opinion/trump-putin-russia-ukraine.html?searchResultPosition=3) disguised as analysis. His diagnosis is spot-on: "Putin keeps spitting in Trump's eye and Trump keeps telling the world that it's raining." But Friedman's remedy is essentially screaming "DO SOMETHING!" at his television. His Chamberlain comparison would make high school debate coaches wince[2](#footnote-2).

**The deeper delusion** both share is their romantic amnesia about pre-war Ukraine: a sham democracy, a primitive post-Soviet feudal autocracy that functioned not through elections or institutions, but through periodic popular uprisings (2004, 2014). Without those revolts, Ukraine would not have existed to be invaded.

The cardboard kids come to mind[3](#footnote-3).

This matters because both authors blithely assume victory leads somewhere obvious. Foreign Affairs envisions Putin's Russia eventually rejoining the "collaborative international frameworks"—presumably after some therapeutic soul-searching. Friedman wants Ukrainian EU membership to solve everything with Brussels bureaucracy.

But what happens after we *win* the war is anyone's guess. Should Ukraine embrace pan-European socialist remedies? Emulate Ireland's Silicon Valley transformation? Neither author bothers asking, content to debate tactics while ignoring the endgame.
