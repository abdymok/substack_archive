I don’t know what to make of the 20-point plan semi-agreed between Team Ukraine and Team USA, with Team Europe’s help.

The problem with armchair strategizing is that it treats survival as an intellectual exercise, a game of hypothetical scenarios where “time” becomes an abstract asset to be “used wisely.”

There is no “wise use of time” when your negotiating partner’s opening position is your non-existence. This is not an exercise in understanding nuance. It’s more about who pays the price for compromising principles and appeasement.
