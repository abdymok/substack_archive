Which brings me to the credibility *snapback* theatre Samuel and Jeremy presented last week, apropos “credible” Ukraine security guarantees: automatic sanctions, weapons surges, financial stabilization funds—all triggered within 48 hours if Russia violates a cease-fire**[1](#footnote-1).**

One problem: legal mechanisms in a lawless world.

**The sanctions fantasy:** China just sold Russia 328,000 miles of fiber-optic cable in a month. Beijing ignores every sanction while NATO writes concerned statements. Charap wants to make this *automatic*? Russia’s already under comprehensive sanctions—they forced Moscow deeper into Chinese/Iranian partnerships that operate entirely outside Western frameworks. You can’t “automatically” reimpose something that never worked.

**The weapons mirage:** The FT [showed](https://www.ft.com/content/f9f42c10-3a30-4ee1-aff7-3368dd831c8c) America *already* providing “route planning, altitude, timing” for Ukrainian strikes through backchannels. Trump *already* threatens Tomahawks. Charap wants to codify what exists precisely *because* formal commitments are impossible. You can’t institutionalize force without law—you just get more lawless force with paperwork.

**The trigger trap:** Russia fires a drone over the border. Ukraine claims violation. European foreign ministers have 48 hours to decide, or sanctions auto-trigger? This isn’t deterrence—it’s a hair-trigger for re-escalation that Russia will never accept and would test constantly if it did.

**The core delusion:** Samuel and Jeremy admit NATO won’t fight Russia, then propose... *different promises*. As if credibility comes from institutional design rather than political will. Poland hits fiscal limits at 4.7% GDP. Europe’s economy stagnates. American support swings with elections. No “standing fund” changes this.

The patient is dead. Stop designing better thermometers.
