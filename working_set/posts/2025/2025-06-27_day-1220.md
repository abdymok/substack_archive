Looking on the bright side, one more prisoner release.

[Image]

The Coordination Headquarters for the Treatment of Prisoners of War said yesterday the exchange took place in accordance with the Istanbul Agreements. A group of defenders under the age of 25, as well as those who were wounded and have health problems, returned to home. The vast majority of those released had been held in captivity for more than three years. Most were captured during the defense of Mariupol in 2022.
