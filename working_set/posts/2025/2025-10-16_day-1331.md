**The Formula That Doesn’t Scale**

Military aid collapsed 43% by summer 2025, right after NATO invented PURL. Eight NATO countries scraped together €1.9 billion for “ready-to-use” weapons. Europe cut its own support by 57%.

Yet… forty percent of Russia’s refining capacity down. Queues stretching kilometers. Crimea rationing fuel at 30 liters per person. By August, 16 of Russia’s 38 refineries struck. Swarms of 300 drones on single targets. Over a million barrels per day offline.

The Americans? “Not acknowledging a direct role.” Just sharing intelligence on targets, altitudes, timing. After Trump’s July call with Z—”could you hit Moscow?”—US intelligence flowed with “new specificity.” Route planning. Air defense mapping. Washington’s “instrument” for strangling the war machine. It works: Russia now imports fuel from Belarus and China.

We’ll be talking about the war with him [Z] and we’ll be talking about -- I mean, they [Ukraine] want to go offensive. **I’ll make a determination on that.** But they would like to go offensive, you know that, and we’ll have to make a determination. - [Trump](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-press-conference-fbi-octoberr-15-2025/) (October 15, 2025)

Now Ukraine shops in Washington. ATACMS. HIMARS. Drone technology sharing. Energy resilience. Weapons procurement dressed as negotiation prep. This is the actual agenda.

Trump, fresh off Gaza, is “optimistic” he can solve Ukraine too. The formula worked—pressure weakened Netanyahu, Hamas folded, ceasefire announced. Surely it scales to Russia?

No. Putin isn’t Netanyahu. He’s not democratically accountable. He’s not desperate for a lifeline.

The White House says Russia “should be motivated” by economic pain. Shambles economy. Virtually no territorial gains. But pain and capitulation aren’t the same thing. Russia is bleeding and still fighting because Putin cannot justify a million soldiers dead or dismembered for nothing. Victory is the only acceptable outcome. Anything less is catastrophic political failure.

Trump doesn’t understand this. He sees weakness and expects compromise. But Putin sees existential stakes.

The tragedy is simpler than anyone admits: Ukraine identified the solution in 2022. Close the 20-fold gap in strike capability or attrition wins. It took until 2025 to act. Now they’re calling it diplomacy. What it actually is: three years late, and framed as leverage for negotiations that were probably never coming anyway.
