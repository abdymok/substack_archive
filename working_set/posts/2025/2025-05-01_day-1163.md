During the summer of 1995 in Minsk I did homework to better understand why so many Belarusians were accommodating dictatorial rule. Every night for two weeks I forced myself to gulp down two, sometimes three, award-winning Dark Prince beers from [Lidskoe Pivo](https://lidskae.by/en/awards/1990-1999/) in Grodno and watch **attentively** the hour-long evening news broadcast by Belarusian State Television (Belarus-1).

This was inspired by the Ludovico Technique from [A Clockwork Orange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Clockwork_Orange_(novel))[1](#footnote-1).

[Video: https://youtu.be/ilNrMr9t7dY]

Donald’s two-hour Cabinet meeting reminded me of my aversion therapy. I imagine people who watched [The Apprentice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Apprentice_(American_TV_series)) (willingly) during the early 2000s were also turned into a clockwork of oranges.

Just skimming through the above video was as enlightening as a [Servant of the People](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Servant_of_the_People_(TV_series)) episode or [Телемарафон](https://uk.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A2%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%84%D0%BE%D0%BD) or a Public Disgrace skit on Kink.com.
