Proposals from [Witkoff-Dmitriev](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/20/trump-ukraine-peace-plan-28-points-russia) vs counter proposals from [Europe](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/full-text-european-counter-proposal-us-ukraine-peace-plan-2025-11-23/):

**Point 10a** - US to receive compensation for the guarantee (new detail)

**Point 10d** - New clause: “If Ukraine launches a missile at Moscow or St. Petersburg without cause, the security guarantee will be deemed invalid” (this appears only in the 28-point plan, actually)

**Point 14** - Completely restructured frozen assets provisions:

- European proposal: Ukraine compensated through frozen Russian assets that remain frozen until Russia pays
- 28-point plan: Specific $100B splits with US getting 50% profits, plus joint US-Russia investment vehicle

## Major Deletions/Changes in European Proposal:

**Point 3** - Explicitly noted as DELETED (the Reuters draft stated expectations that Russia won’t invade neighbors and NATO won’t expand)

**Point 6** - Military size cap changed from **600,000** to **800,000** personnel

**Point 7** - Significantly softened:

- 28-point: Ukraine constitutionally banned from NATO, NATO statutorily prevented from admitting Ukraine
- European: “Ukraine joining NATO depends on consensus of NATO members, which does not exist” (much weaker language)

**Point 8** - Weakened from absolute ban to conditional:

- 28-point: “NATO agrees not to station troops in Ukraine”
- European: “NATO agrees not to permanently station troops **under its command** in Ukraine **in peacetime**“ (allows temporary/wartime deployments)

**Point 9** - Changed from “European fighter jets” to “NATO fighter jets”

**Point 10** - Missing the Moscow/St. Petersburg missile clause from 28-point plan

**Point 15** - Restructured:

- 28-point: US-Russian bilateral working group
- European: Multilateral taskforce including US, Ukraine, Russia, **and Europeans**

**Point 20** - Dramatically reduced:

- 28-point: Extensive mutual educational programs, media rights, anti-Nazi provisions for both countries
- European: Only requires Ukraine to adopt EU rules on religious tolerance and linguistic minorities (Russia obligations removed)

**Point 21** - Completely rewritten territories section:

- 28-point: Specific recognition of Crimea, Luhansk, Donetsk as Russian; frozen status for Kherson/Zaporizhia; demilitarized buffer zone in Donetsk
- European: Vague “negotiations on territorial swaps will start from Line of Contact” with commitment not to recover territory militarily (no specific territorial concessions listed)

**Point 25** - Timeline change:

- 28-point: Elections in 100 days
- European: Elections “as soon as possible”

**Point 26** - Amnesty clause DELETED entirely from European proposal (28-point included full amnesty for all wartime actions)

**Point 27** - Name change: “Peace Council” → “Board of Peace”

In summary, the European proposal appears significantly **more favorable to Ukraine** than the 28-point plan:

- Larger military allowed (800K vs 600K)
- NATO membership language much softer
- No specific territorial concessions spelled out
- No amnesty for Russian actions
- Frozen assets go to Ukraine, not split with US/Russia joint ventures
- More multilateral oversight (Europeans included)
- Russia gets fewer specific concessions on education/cultural policies
