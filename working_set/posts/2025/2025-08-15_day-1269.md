# Two Jackasses, One Map Collection

*Neither side will open the other's doors or ride in the other's vehicles. If 10 US agents are posted outside a meeting room, 10 Russian agents will stand on the other side. Everything is matched body for body, gun for gun.* — [Secret Service Rushes to Alaska City for Trump-Putin Summit](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-14/secret-service-rushes-to-make-alaska-city-safe-for-trump-putin?srnd=homepage-europe) (Bloomberg, August 14, 2025)

After years of winking, pinches and strategic silence… anchorage.

Last week, Trump casually swatted away accusations of rolling over for Putin—while handing over concessions Putin couldn't extract through years of warfare. Just like that. No ceremony, no drama.

Putin can show up practically empty-handed. Well, almost. Word is he's packing "historical materials"—geographical maps to prove Ukraine is an "artificial state." No ceasefire offers. No retraction of pseudo-historical fantasies. Just props.

Oh, and strategic nukes, Arctic rare earths, waterfront development projects. The usual.

Meanwhile, Ukraine and Europe's frantic attempts to crash this party? Complete washout. Trump and Putin will chat about whatever strikes their fancy—including Putin's greatest hits of geographical fiction—while everyone else stews.

Tomorrow's meeting is marinated in subjectivity but locked into hard realities nobody can wave away. Putin showing up with a briefcase full of maps like some deranged geography teacher? Pure theatrical flair.

The pre-meeting pronouncements from world leaders? Theater for the home crowds. Diplomatic "sleep tight, all is well." What Z says matters exactly as much as what Trump, Merz, or Starmer says. Putin's got the right instinct: shut up and let everyone assume you're brilliant. Or at least pack visual aids.

Putin's bringing what Russians call "one of the most representative foreign delegations in Russia's history"—Lavrov, Belousov, Siluanov, the works. Trump will match with his own A-team, minus Pete, [who is in the dog house again](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/aug/14/pete-hegseth-pentagon-christian-nationalism), this time for foisting his religious zealotry on American soldiers ordered to police US cities.

## The Real Game

Putin wants his deal—but only with proper Western genuflection. He'll take partial genuflection for now. His ace? Things are heating up but not quite on fire, so he can drag this out for better terms. The nightmare? Trump bails, taking the golden opportunity with him.

Trump's ready to fold, mainly because he's convinced folding isn't folding. This is Biden's mess. Biden takes the loss. Trump wants to trade Ukrainian real estate for Russian friendship—the "more valuable prize." Problem: his people aren't ready to give away all of Ukraine. Tough sell.

Two guys desperately want to consummate this thing, but Ukrainians keep cockblocking them. Tomorrow they'll try to muscle through the obstacles—Putin armed with questionable cartography—but they probably won't have enough runway to close the deal.

We're watching history pivot on Trump’s bullshit and Putin's map collection.

The war grinds on. People die.
