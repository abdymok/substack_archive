# The Absurdist War Economy

So a casting agency pivots to drones and suddenly it’s got $1 billion in contracts. Fire Point went from scouting film locations for Z’s rom-coms to churning out exploding Styrofoam birds at thirty secret factories. The owner still runs a location scouting business. The CTO used to make concrete patio furniture. Now they’re building the Flamingo—a jet-powered drone with a one-ton warhead that got its name because they ran out of red paint. Z promises 3,000 of them this year. You cannot make this up[1](#footnote-1).

Meanwhile, Putin’s playing the long game of making Ukraine uninhabitable. His new Shahed UAV’s—now three times faster, AI-guided, dive-bombing from above—come in waves of 600 or 700 a night. They’re systematically dismantling the power grid, substation by substation, trying to cut the industrial east off from energy production in the west. The goal isn’t conquest anymore. It’s depopulation. Make it so cold and dark that millions simply leave.

Ukraine’s counter-strategy is the mirror image: blow up Russia’s refineries until the economy collapses first. Fire Point claims its drones now conduct 60% of Ukraine’s strikes inside Russia. Fuel shortages are spreading. But there’s a catch—most drones get intercepted, so you need volume, which means keeping them cheap, which apparently means tolerating quality issues and skipping price negotiations with companies run by people who made garden benches last year.

Under the diplomatic carpet, the bulldogs gnaw. Putin wants Z gone—not the man, the symbol. Trump doesn’t want to be Putin’s errand boy but also doesn’t want to fund this forever. So they circle the airfield, unable to land, while Fire Point paints another batch of Flamingos and Kyiv’s metro goes dark for the first time ever.

By January, this war will have lasted longer than the Soviet Union fought Nazi Germany. Russia’s conquered an additional 1% of Ukraine at the cost of a million casualties. The drone revolution means neither side can make major gains. So they’ll just keep bombing each other’s infrastructure until somebody’s economy collapses first.
