[Putin’s having a moment](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/putin-says-tomahawk-supply-ukraine-would-destroy-us-relations-2025-10-05/). Tomahawk missiles to Ukraine would “destroy” U.S.-Russia relations, he warns—apparently bombing Lviv, flying drones over Danish oilfields, and hacking Norwegian dams are all fine for “positive trends” in bilateral bonhomie, but giving Kyiv 1,550-mile-range cruise missiles crosses some imaginary red line only visible from the Kremlin.

[Image]

The timing? *Chef’s kiss.* Less than two months after Trump’s Alaska summit, where presumably everyone shook hands and pretended this wasn’t a shitshow, Putin is reduced to flying fibre optic drones into Kramatorsk and blowing up random sedans. If Russia truly had any conventional superiority, he’d be advancing. Instead, war crimes.

As we pointed out on Friday, this *is* a military dead end[1](#footnote-1). Attrition assumes grinding down works. History says it doesn’t.

Stalemate is attrition’s confession of failure.

Russia can’t make even one step ahead in Ukraine, so Moscow pivots to grey-zone theater: sabotage, airspace violations, the whole repertoire of weakness masquerading as intimidation. Cheap drones have frozen the battlefield into deadlock. Russia’s flying 34,000 drones into Ukraine this year—nine times more than last—but it’s desperation math, not dominance.

[The myth that only American aid matters](https://www.kielinstitut.de/topics/war-against-ukraine/ukraine-support-tracker/). European contributions exceed US support. Russia’s manageable *if confronted*. But first, apparently, Team USA needs to negotiate whether giving Ukraine the ability to shoot back at the country bombing its cities is compatible with Putin’s feelings.
