The president said he was ready to talk about holding elections, but Ukrainians "absolutely do not want" them because of the possible risk to Ukraineʼs security.

> "They are afraid. We will lose the legal regime of martial law if there are elections. The soldiers will return home, and Putin will occupy all of our territory. > "And if someone doesnʼt like it, they can choose another citizenship. If they want. They have property abroad," Z said.
