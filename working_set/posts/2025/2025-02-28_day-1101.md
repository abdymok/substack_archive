[Image]

It shows the American people that there is upside here for them, that we have not squandered the money because the American people are always concerned about overseas intervention.”

We are all, of course, in awe of Scott’s courage to visit Kyiv to weeks ago, but his conclusion that Donald will make history by “making assets for the American people rather than debt in Ukraine” is, er, bullshit.

While there are indeed rare earth minerals in Ukraine, geologists do not know where they are, quantity, how much they’re worth or how much it will cost to extract them to the surface.
