We're all familiar with the cosmological anthropic principle—the woo-ish argument made by some scientists turned amateur philosophers that if not God, then *something* must have fine-tuned the fundamental constants of the universe to allow for life.

As a professional expert on Russia, Belarus, and Ukraine, I've noticed something troubling among some *influencers* who venture into my field. These people seem to believe that different rules about facts and debate apply when they become amateur experts about our bloody little war.

Stay-behind saboteur in Belarus during World War II Valentyn Taras in the day captured this perfectly when asked about the resistance movement—he described academic speculation abroad about stay-behind saboteurs in Belarus as "playing historical tennis without a net."

[Video: https://youtu.be/sgfy9lans1s]

- Claims fly everywhere
- No rules to determine what's \*in\* or \*out\*
- Historical \*facts\* scatter wildly
- No way to score legitimate points

It's time we put some nets back up.
