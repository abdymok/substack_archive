So, we can’t rule out that Russia has entered the precision-strike phase of its genocidal war. For now, we’ll go with the simpler explanation.

**Occam's razor analysis:**

The simpler explanation requires fewer assumptions:

- Russia launched missiles at a central Kyiv location
- The area happened to contain media and international offices (along with many other buildings)
- Missiles hit their intended coordinates with high precision

The more complex explanation would require additional assumptions:

- Russia specifically researched and mapped media/international office locations
- Russia made a strategic decision to target these specific types of organizations
- Russia chose this particular moment to escalate to "precision-strike phase of genocidal war"
- The targeting was intentionally symbolic rather than tactically motivated
