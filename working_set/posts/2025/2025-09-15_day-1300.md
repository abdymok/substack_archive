# Russia Invades Polish Airspace, So Trump Issues an Ultimatum to... NATO

Russian drones crossed into Polish airspace last week. NATO territory. Article 5 stuff.

Trump's response? [Lecture Europe about their energy bills](https://x.com/WhiteHouse/status/1966846399355732034).

## The Art of the Misdeal

Donald says Z and Vova hate each other too much to talk directly. So he'll play middleman. "Somehow we'll get it settled," [he said](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9S8VVOZXzk).

His mediation strategy? Threatening to abandon the table unless NATO does exactly what he wants. His September 13th letter reads like a ransom note written by someone who learned diplomacy on X:

> "I am ready to do major Sanctions on Russia when all NATO Nations have agreed, and started, to do the same thing, and when all NATO Nations STOP BUYING OIL FROM RUSSIA... NATO'S commitment to WIN has been far less than 100%, and the purchase of Russian Oil, by some, has been shocking!... If NATO does as I say, the WAR will end quickly... If not, you are just wasting my time, and the time, energy, and money of the United States."

This dropped three days after 19 Russian drones violated Polish territory. While NATO allies were still *processing* the escalation, Trump was busy scolding them about "shocking" energy purchases.

Meanwhile, on Saturday Secretary Rubio [parsed](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2025/09/secretary-of-state-marco-rubio-remarks-to-press-2/) whether the drone incursion was "targeted" or merely "intentional"—as if there's a meaningful difference when your neighbor's military hardware is buzzing around your backyard uninvited.

## Putin's Pavlovian Response

While Trump threatens to waste everyone's "time, energy, and money," Russia has systematically ramped up strikes since he took office, according to [The New York Times](http://nyt.com).

The pattern is grimly predictable: Russia escalates immediately after diplomatic talks. It's like Putin has diplomacy-triggered Tourette's, but with missiles.

- After May's Istanbul cease-fire talks? Moscow intensified airstrikes.
- After Trump and Putin's July phone call? Russia launched 728 drones and 13 missiles less than a week later. *Thanks for the chat, Don!*
- The August Alaska summit brought temporary quiet—just long enough for Russia to stockpile drones for the deadliest Kyiv assault of the war.

Even Putin's ceasefires come with assembly required.

[Image]

Russia now produces 2,700 attack drones monthly, up from the old days of buying them from Iran like discount Ikea furniture. In July, they launched 6,300 drones against Ukraine. Last July? 426.

That's not an escalation. That's Black Friday for war crimes.

Since Trump took office, Ukrainian civilians have endured three of the deadliest months of air raids on record. But sure, let's talk about Europe's energy bills.

## The Wrong Target

So Russian drones violate NATO territory—potentially triggering Article 5—and Trump issues ultimatums to... the victims and their allies.

Not to Putin, who's running a drone assembly line like he's prepping for the apocalypse.

Not to Moscow, whose drones just treated Polish airspace like a scenic route.

But to European allies whose commitment levels apparently fall short of his exacting customer service standards.

This is dealmaking as performance art: create artificial deadlines, threaten to walk away, blame everyone except the guy literally bombing hospitals, and expect breakthrough results from theatrical tantrums.

Those 6,300 monthly Russian drones keep launching. Polish and Romanian airspace keeps getting violated.

But hey, at least NATO knows exactly where they stand with their "time-wasting" energy policies.
