Here’s the thing about getting expelled from Belarus for “interfering in internal affairs.” It was 1997, and we had been running the third sector like liberalism-peddling maniacs since ‘94, helping build things—legal libraries, high school debate teams, independent media, history books, internet connectivity, etc. Real money for real people doing real work that made the country’s potato-shaped dictator sweat through his polyester suit.

The photo tells you everything: me flanked by the chairman of the Supreme Economic Court, a couple of state security goons who looked like they’d flunked out of villain school, the foreign minister, and US Ambassador Kenneth Yalowitz. Classic post-Soviet kabuki—everyone pretending this was diplomacy while the secret police took notes for my deportation file.

> Free money,

> can turn anyone into a target.

Same playbook Lukashenko used: foreign interference, destabilizing activities, funding opposition. Except now it’s coming from Washington, not some third-world capital with pretensions. Pam, Kash, and that prick Aakash have stumbled ass-backwards into adopting the paranoid fantasies of every tin-pot dictator who couldn’t handle a few lawyers learning how to read contracts.

The network’s transition to US politics, pencils and oranges—safe, neutered, committee-approved programming—coincided with my departure. I looked at their website recently and nearly fell asleep scrolling through the 2000s. The conspicuous absence of 1995 from [the timeline](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/who-we-are/our-history) is particularly rich, since that year was peak disruption, when informed citizens who knew their rights weren’t afraid to use them.

What’s darkly hilarious is watching American officials discover the same paranoia that made Lukashenko shit his pants. They’re not just targeting Soros—they’re taking a blowtorch to America’s own liberalism promotion apparatus while genuflecting to every strongman who ever whined about criticism. The mouth-breathing security operatives in that ‘96 photo knew exactly what they were looking at: a threat to their pathetic little fiefdom.

The real tragedy? Team Trump has reached the same moronic conclusion.
