Not much left of [Bilozerske](https://deepstatemap.live/#12/48.5044360/37.0816040), population 14,000, where it has been raining KAB bombs for the last couple of days. KAB is short for Korrektiruyemaya Aviatsionnaya Bomba, which translates to "Correctable Aerial Bomb." These are Soviet-era bombs upgraded with extendable wings and a guidance system. Russian pilots launch them at the edge of Ukrainian airspace and fly home as the bomb glides toward its target.

He casually mentions "land swapping" and notes that Russia has grabbed "largely... oceanfront property" because, as any good mogul knows, waterfront is always prime. One can almost hear him appraising Crimea: "Beautiful location, great views, recently renovated by new management."

Most deliciously absurd is Donald's complaint that Z requires "constitutional approval" for territorial concessions—imagine that, a democracy requiring democratic processes!—while simultaneously boasting that he personally "stopped the biggest pipeline in the world" and gave Ukraine "tank busters" instead of Obama's "sheets." It's Peter Pan bragging about both saving and abandoning the Lost Boys, depending on which sounds more heroic.

The whole affair reeks of two larger than life jerks who've painted themselves into their respective corners. Vova, having bitten off more Ukrainian territory than he can apparently digest, suddenly discovers diplomacy. TACO Donald, having promised to end the war in a day, now speaks of "feel out meetings" and "complex" situations requiring multiple European consultations.

When Donald mentions there will be "some swapping" of territory and describes Russia's conquests as "very prime territory... oceanfront property," we're witnessing something unprecedented: a real estate developer treating sovereign nations like a Monopoly board while millions await their fate.

The real tragedy isn't that Peter Pan and Captain Hook are meeting in Alaska—it's that they're discussing Ukrainian lives and territory as if it were just another ratings opportunity. Pass the pixie dust—we're all going to need it.
