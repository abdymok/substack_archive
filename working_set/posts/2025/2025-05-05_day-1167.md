“How are you to build a future if you have forgotten who you were before?” is the question we mulled over the weekend — the heart of the existential crisis confronting millions in/from this part of the world.

The quandary is not only personal but also emblematic of the gramaphobic murkiness of the first quarter of the 21st century.

- The struggle of memory against political and personal oblivion.
- The existential challenge of exile and the search for belonging.
- The pain of irretrievable loss and the impossibility of fully reclaiming the past.

People adrift and looking back, looking only back, seeing what’s far behind them.
