I came across at least six burnt cars in my photo roll over the last three months, so, yeah, it’s a problem.

[Image]

The arsonists are usually poor young people — many not even adults — hired over social media platforms by Russian agents. [According to Ukraine’s Security Service](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=whGN520fqE4&t=15s), nearly one in five Ukrainians recruited is underage.
