I have written sparingling about my 3-year vacation in Berlin during the early 1980s because, er, the subject here is not *комильфо*[2](#footnote-2)[3](#footnote-3)[4](#footnote-4)[5](#footnote-5)*.* In other words, it’s *malappris*, excuse my French.

So what is Keith smoking?

I suspect Z and his potent managers know zero about post-war occupied Germany because they are bad students of history. Sympathetic army of occupation vets might clue them in to the joys of life in Berlin under the Four Powers Agreement and Allied Control Council — a terrible model to resurrect for Ukraine. Many French and English vets fictionalized their experience of being stationed in Berlin. They were a motley bunch, for the most part poorly fed and unequipped (to deal with their Soviet adversaries).

As for the Americans, there was Det A, but I digress.

Last time I checked, Keith was commander of SOCEUR after the decapitation of Yugoslavia. During the Cold War he was doing push ups and teaching kids how to jump out of airplanes.
