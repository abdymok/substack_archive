Three years ago today, Russia declared its annexation of four Ukrainian regions: Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson and Zaporizhia, roughly the size of the state of Virginia. If we add Crimea, which Russia annexed in 2014, it’s the size of Alabama, a little more than 52,000 square miles. The Kremlin has published Putin’s congratulatory address on the anniversary of the *reunification*.

[Image]

The 47th President promised he’d end the war “in 24 hours” 53 times on the campaign trail. Instead, after fawning over Putin with a red-carpet welcome in Alaska this summer, Trump has delivered exactly what his critics predicted: zero progress, maximum spectacle, and a war now in its 12th year.

---

Yesterday’s lengthy interview with Kherson Mayor Volodymyr Mykolaienko, who was released after three years of torture in a Russian prison this summer, details how quickly the Rusians seized Kherson in February 2022[1](#footnote-1).

> It was very painful to watch how in a few hours they were already standing near Kherson, on the left bank. There was confusion. How can this be? After all, only weeks earlier [[February 12, 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEybCJtW7Gc)], Z visited, attended exercises [tactical and special exercises organized by the Ministry of Internal Affairs]. They told him everything was fine. The president even held a press conference and said officials were not worried [about a Russia invasion], but we all saw what happened next…

[Image]

Remember when Z confidently assured Ukrainians that “the Russian people will not go to war against the Ukrainian people”? That was February 19, 2022—just five days before the invasion. Tragically, many Ukrainians in southeastern parts of the country believed him and didn’t evacuate.

This catastrophic intelligence failure—or willful denial—cost tens of thousands of Ukrainian lives in Mariupol alone, not to mention the tens of thousands of Ukrainian children kidnapped and deported to Russia. Cities that could have been evacuated became death traps. Families that might have fled stayed put, trusting their president’s assurances right up until Russian tanks rolled in.

It remains one of the most devastating miscalculations of the war, and Z has never adequately answered for it.
