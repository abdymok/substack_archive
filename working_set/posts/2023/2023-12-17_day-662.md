Actually, no. Ukraine’s failure to get its forced conscription act together has been common knowledge long before the start of the Great War. The first three defense ministers under Z since 2019 — Zahorodniuk, Taran, Reznikov — didn’t fix it, and it’s an open question whether Umerov — the fourth — wants to, can or will.

The only way to make things better – short of re-razing Veranda on the River and cleaning up the Dnipro so that more beavers swim there again[2](#footnote-2) – is to dwell on the positive, count your blessings, and know that [Venediktova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iryna_Venediktova) has been gaining a ton of weight since her appointment as ambassador to Switzerland.
