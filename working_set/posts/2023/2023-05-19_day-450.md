**UPDATE:** We left off [yesterday](https://abdymok.substack.com/p/day-449) whining about Musk and Magneto as enemy cruise missiles hurtled towards Kyiv. About 30 minutes later, the rockets, mostly Kh-101s, arrived, travelling at around 600 mph. The Air Force shot them all down, except one.

About 23 hours later finds us in a time loop. We’ve been stuck in the same day, over and over again, for most of this week. Russia obviously intends to continue shooting at us until they hit something.
