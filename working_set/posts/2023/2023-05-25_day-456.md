I turned off my mind and dropped by, accompanied by the guy with tattoos on his neck holding what looked to be the Ukrainian version of a [Box turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_turtle).

The three paintings I liked the best were ““On the Way Home,” “Searching for the Truth,” and “Fireworks.”

“Fireworks” is English for Salyut (Салют), the name of [the iconic Kyiv hotel](https://socialistmodernism.com/hotel-salyut-kiev/) overlooking Park Slava. Anyone who remembers Craine International Trading, Inc. will recall the carpets on the 4th floor of this architectural monument/den of thieves (circa 1991).

[Image]
