Gosh, thickest Summer. It’s nice right now: We’re swimming backstroke in a big pool of quiet. And music. The Chornobyl exclusion zone wildfires have died down and dacha life will be extended until at least October. The roads are slow with Land Rover sedans pulling caravans full of kids to and from summer camps. My Kyiv neighborhood is overrun now and again, this weekend by people wearing athleisure to avoid heat stroke.

Sweet chestnuts are ripening, because the sun is relentless. The dogs, they pant.

Progress is impossible without change, and those who cannot change their minds cannot change anything.

**[George Bernard Shaw](https://www.azquotes.com/author/13418-George_Bernard_Shaw)**

Basically what Eliot complains about at [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/international/archive/2023/08/ukraine-western-diplomacy/674920/).

During this part of the summer, Sirius is the brightest star in the Canis Major (Large Dog) constellation. I had intended to write something about Wagner PMC, but was distracted by Eliot’s essay. The occasion of Days of Dog Star is more or less 20 days prior and 20 days after the star of Sirius rises and falls in conjunction with the sun. The history of ancient Egypt, [the goddess Nut](https://landioustravel.com/egypt/egyptian-deities/goddess-nut/), is relevant, but I digress.
