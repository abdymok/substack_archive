Busy night in Kyiv. Lots of fireworks, drones, missiles, hunks of burning stuff falling from the sky landing wherever. Several buildings were hit and partially destroyed.

Russia started sending missiles to Kyiv regularly to kill civilians on October 10, 2022[1](#footnote-1). That was about a month *after* we started writing about Schwerpunkt[2](#footnote-2).

Kori in Washington, D.C. has started to complain again, but Uncle Joe says long-range ATACMs are still in play[3](#footnote-3)[4](#footnote-4).

In other news, OK Wine tells me I’ve forked over about UAH 10,000 for various beverages (mostly Saperavi and Mukuzani) over the last six months.

Not nearly enough.
