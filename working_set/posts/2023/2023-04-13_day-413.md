**Hundreds (100s)** — up to 1,000 — of classified Pentagon briefs, mostly dealing with Ukraine, were uploaded to a Discord server **since October**, according to Aric Toler, who chatted with members of the group involved in the megaleak ([@lawfare](https://www.lawfareblog.com/lawfare-podcast-rid-and-toler-latest-megaleak)).

John wrote about the [Defense Intelligence Agency](https://www.dia.mil/)’s assessment of the bloody mess based on materials appearing online[1](#footnote-1). But what John didn’t say is that no one in their right mind takes anything the DIA reports about Ukraine seriously, at least not in Ukraine[2](#footnote-2).

Shane and Samuel at The Washington Post tell [the best story so far](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2023/04/12/discord-leaked-documents/) about the leaks and alleged leaker, known as Og to his disciples[3](#footnote-3).

We assume Og has been identified and is in custody or dead as of this writing.

OG used to mean [Original Gangster](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=Original%20Gangster) although some [people](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=poeple) these days use OG as a [quicker](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=quicker) way of saying Original.
