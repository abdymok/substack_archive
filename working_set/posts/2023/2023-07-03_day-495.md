I didn’t read the latest reportage because I was distracted by Erin Burnett [chatting up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zzJzEvaL5A0&ab_channel=CNN) Mike Pence after his pow wow w/ Z.

Erin later visited Andriivka, a small town west of Kyiv.

[Video: https://youtu.be/Cv7O8DAGsZs]

This morning I learned that Victoria Amelina, and a dozen more people, died in the missile strike last week on the Ria Lounge pizzeria in Kramatorsk.

[Image]

Victoria published her first novel, *The November Syndrom, or Homo Compatiens,* in 2014. She became a war crimes war crimes researcher this spring and joined the human rights organization [Truth Hounds](https://truth-hounds.org/en/homepage/).

A [friend of mine](https://www.instagram.com/p/CuDH_XcsR4z/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igshid=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==) was at the pizzeria an hour before the missile hit. He went back and took pictures of the horror.
