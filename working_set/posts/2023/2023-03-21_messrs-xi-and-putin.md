In other news, [the World Health Organization estimates that as many as 9.6 million Ukrainians may have a mental health condition](https://www.who.int/news-room/feature-stories/detail/scaling-up-mental-health-and-psychosocial-services-in-war-affected-regions--best-practices-from-ukraine#:~:text=Ukraine's%20current%20mental%20health%20situation,disorder%2C%20bipolar%20disorder%20or%20schizophrenia.), of whom 3.9 million may have *needs* that are moderate or severe.

That’s what I thought about during the run this morning, *needs*.

[Image]

Unfortunately, many of those affected own automobiles and are aggressive drivers. Some resent bicyclists in principle and run over pedestrians at crosswalks for the heck of it. These drivers always think they have the right of way, even when it’s very early in the morning and traffic is light.

[Image]

I stopped counting years ago how many times cars and trucks have almost maimed or killed me. The closest call with permanent debilitating injury or death came when a truck clipped my left handlebar on the road to Hoholiv, [a former Cossack town](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Hoholiv,+Kyiv+Oblast,+07452/@50.520072,31.0109246,13z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x40d4dffc6583164d:0x5e25e8870fc225d0!8m2!3d50.509297!4d31.0160571!16s%2Fm%2F0sgfr_d).
