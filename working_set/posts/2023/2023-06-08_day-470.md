We received detailed instructions on WhatsApp from Ed between visits to Kyiv’s main synagogue, [Tootsie Club](https://en.tootsie.kiev.ua/) and umbrellas at Golden Gate. That’s Ed from smokey New York City, from the Yugoslav wars and Day 21[1](#footnote-1). He worked in some of the most dangerous places in the world, including Sudan, Sierra Leone, Afghanistan, Iraq, Iran, Yemen, Northern Ireland, Sarajevo, Kosovo, and Haiti.

One of the mottos [Chien-Chi Chang](https://www.google.com/search?q=chien-chi+chang&oq=chien&aqs=chrome.0.35i39i355i650j46i39i650j69i57j46i10i340i512j0i10i512j69i60l3.1299j0j4&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8) and I have been kicking around during walks around Kyiv this week — “Out with the old, In with the new.” We also contemplate *panopticon* as a theme in the 2019-2020 Hong Kong protests.

FYI about [Tootsie Club](https://en.tootsie.kiev.ua/), new Martial Law hours: doors open at 4 p.m. and close at 9 p.m., until the war ends.
