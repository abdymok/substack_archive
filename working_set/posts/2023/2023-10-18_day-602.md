I watched the vote for a Speaker. Jim Jordan did not get the votes (217) necessary for the job. There will be another vote today.

Yesterday’s [Al-Ahli Baptist Hospital disaster](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2023/10/17/world/gaza-news-israel-hamas-war/heres-the-latest-on-the-war?smid=url-share) in Gaza reminded me of Russia’s war crimes in Ukraine. Last time I checked, officials had registered more than **[100,000 potential war](https://carnegieendowment.org/2023/09/26/ukraine-s-quest-for-justice-conversation-with-prosecutor-general-andriy-kostin-event-8161#:~:text=In%20the%20past%20year%20and,Russian%20troops%20on%20Ukrainian%20soil.)****[crimes](https://carnegieendowment.org/2023/09/26/ukraine-s-quest-for-justice-conversation-with-prosecutor-general-andriy-kostin-event-8161#:~:text=In%20the%20past%20year%20and,Russian%20troops%20on%20Ukrainian%20soil.)** committed in Ukraine by Russia, including missile strikes on civilian targets, e.g. hospitals, shopping malls, residential buildings.

Since Russia invaded in 2014, the UN has done a [shitty job](https://www.ohchr.org/en/ohchr_homepage) documenting them. [WikipediA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_crimes_in_the_Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) has done better. The [ICC](https://www.icc-cpi.int/) is investigating, but no one is holding their breath.
