In other news, the maniacs next door early this morning lobbed another salvo of land-based Iskander ballistic cruise missiles at Kyiv. We think. Collateral damage was impressive. More than 50 people injured, hospital windows blown out, water main pipe ruptured, [et cetera](https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=868011378350949).

The Iskander is a Cold War relic. They weigh about 4,000 kilograms and carry a 500 kilogram warhead. Russia launches them from Bryansk region, which means they don’t have to cover much distance. Pretty much straight up and straight down.
