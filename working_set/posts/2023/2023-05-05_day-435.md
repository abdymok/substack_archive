[Video: https://youtu.be/ZT0BnrxbO9g]

Kyiv mayor Vitali Klitschko said firefighters put out a fire on the ground floor of a four-storey shopping centre in the Solomyansky district where the drone crashed. Ukraine’s Air Force an hour later on Telegram [apologized](https://t.me/kpszsu/2499) for the screw-up and said it would investigate the cause of the malfunction.
