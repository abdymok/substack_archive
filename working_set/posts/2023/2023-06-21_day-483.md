Some time in the last couple weeks, while working on a gizzed-up translation of Ukraine’s balance of payments and exchange rate forecast (you know, the macroeconomic analysis I’ve been updating and alluding to in previous posts for [the past, er, five years](https://www.ukraine-economic-outlook.com/researches), about the unfettered [laissez-faire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laissez-faire) mess that is not running like a finely tuned and greased machine, the one I decided to describe to the English-speaking world, whereupon I was seized by a strange and insistent demon who was of the opinion that simply doing one thing and doing it well was not enough, boy, you need to employ a team of seasoned psychologists that would do several things in a kind of backoffice so-so sort of way but it sure would impress the twelve people at [Blackrock](https://www.ft.com/content/3d6041fb-5747-4564-9874-691742aa52a2) to whom such therapy would even make any sense, and whereupon I tapped out lines of inelegant Monetary Base hooey until droplets of blood formed on my forehead and I was hoarse from screaming *well why the fuck not* at a computer screen every time something refused to work – and of course things don’t work during war, things don’t like to work – and on it went until I was sufficiently unstupid to pause and grasp that having something that does one thing well is a good deal better than having many things that are just sort of so-so, and hey there’s all this time in the future to add those things when and if they *do* work, and I began the relatively swift process of dismantling all those flights of financial fantasy until I arrived at the point I am now, which is ready to publish updates about the mess) I re-discovered the [Kyiv School of Economics](https://kse.ua/), which is a unfiscal mess.

I then stumbled on Timofiy Mylovanov and accidentally read this long [threaded tweet](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1670936455764475906.html) about **HIMSELF** on the occasion of the [Ukraine Recovery Conference](https://www.urc-international.com/) in London. And became queasy.

# “The threat is real.”

In fact, this is very old news, which we dispensed with on Day 14[1](#footnote-1). This fear has obviously been percolating in the American president’s head, along with [unease](https://www.washingtonpost.com/climate-environment/2023/05/22/colorado-river-water-conservation-deal-states/) that the Colorado River might dry up.

I was also unimpressed by the bits about BAF and Belarus (pp. 47-48).
