But back to the war.

> Today’s actions around Robotyne are likely the start of any “main *thrust*” Ukrainian forces might be launching, if the US officials are correct, rather than the sum of such a *thrust*. — [Institute for the Study of War](https://understandingwar.org/backgrounder/russian-offensive-campaign-assessment-july-26-2023) (July 26, 2023)

I suppose it all depends what, exactly, the ISW’s war analysts mean by *thrust*. So, I checked on their website, where I bumped into [Nataliya’s latest op-ed](https://www.understandingwar.org/backgrounder/ukraine%E2%80%99s-sustained-counteroffensive-denying-russia%E2%80%99s-prolongation-war). It’s footnoted 15 times to various ISW reports, which are [chock full](https://www.understandingwar.org/search/google/thrust) of *thrusting*.

Yeech!

This reminded me again of Fred and friends and their loopy counterfactual from December 2021.
