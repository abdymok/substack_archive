Annotated Bibliography

Working draft · 2023–2026 · compiled October 31, 2026 · 307 publisher-year entries

Dates attached to individual source citations in the annual research notes and audit follow the date the item first appeared in Kyiv time (Europe/Kyiv). Original publication timestamps are converted to Kyiv time where available; later updates and syndication dates are not substituted. If only a publisher calendar date is available, that original date is retained and material ambiguity is flagged. Date ranges below describe when a publisher was cited in the archive, not necessarily the publisher’s publication dates.

This bibliography lists recurring external sources cited in the archive: a publisher or source is included for a given year when it appears in two or more posts during that year. Links back to the author's own archive and sites are excluded. The same publisher may appear in more than one year because the counts and annotations describe that year's use.

The four annual link files contain 5,438 link occurrences across 976 posts with at least one link. The combined source registry identifies 853 source domains, 812 normalized publishers and 4,218 distinct URL strings. Counts are link occurrences and distinct posts, not unique articles, readership or influence. Publisher aliases are consolidated using the archive's source registry. Representative links are examples, not an exhaustive list of every item cited.

Annotations identify the source category and describe how the archive uses it. They do not rate reliability or imply endorsement. The 2023 section retains the original editorial approach, with publisher aliases and counts reconciled against the source registry and BelTA added as a separate state-media source. The 2024–2026 sections use the same core categories, with a separate state-media category where relevant. Source categories are drawn from the normalized registry. The domain-alias map documents 134 source-domain to publisher mappings. The 299 remaining unclassified domains each occur once, so no unclassified source meets the two-post inclusion threshold.

The 2026 link dataset runs through October 28. It contains 1,132 link records spanning 218 post-day labels. The recovered 26_redact.md contains 247 posts (1.29 MB of text); individual post files are also present in working_set/posts/2026/ (247 files).

Related annual research drafts: 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. These are separate, more selective article-level notes. Data files: working_set.
